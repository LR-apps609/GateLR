Varchi (ZTL gates) and hours
- The Comune's SIRIO system has been in place since 2007 and reads plates at the gates. "La città di Ravenna è dotata dal 2007 del sistema SIRIO per la rilevazione automatica degli accessi in ZTL."
- The Comune's list of gates shows six active around the clock: via di Roma, via Guaccimanni, via Baccarini, via Rondinelli, via Cavour (entrance from piazzetta Gandhi), and via Ponte Marino. Three run 7:30–20:30: via Mariani, via Guerrini, and via Matteucci. (GUERRINI DALLE ORE 07.30 ALLE ORE 20.30 VIA PONTE MARINO 24 ORE SU 24 VIA P. MATTEUCCI DALLE ORE 07.30 ALLE ORE 20.30)
- Conflict: Accessibilitacentristorici lists via Ponte Marino as 7:30–20:30, not 24 hours ("via Mariani, via Guerrini, Ponte marino, via Matteucci sono in funzione tutti i giorni dalle ore 7:30 alle ore 20:30"). The Comune page appears to say 24 hours.
- A 2026 guide notes that the Comune does not say which days the 7:30–20:30 bands apply, and that the rules may have changed in 2026. (Autovia)
Commercial vehicle hours (possibly outdated): CNA Ravenna: loading and unloading dalle 3 alle 10 e dalle 14 alle 16; Accessibilitacentristorici: dalle 04.00 alle 10.30 e dalle 14.30 alle 16.00, 30-minute parking disc.
Permits
- The daily ZTL permit is issued 24 hours a day from ticket machines (parcometri) for vehicles up to 5 tonnes. Heavier vehicles need a separate nulla osta from the Servizio Strade. (Comune ZTL page)
- The permits office is at via D'Alaggio 3, tel. 0544 482937, open dal lunedì al venerdì dalle 9.00 alle 12.30.
- Accessibilitacentristorici gives a daily permit price of 8 euro (page about 2,561 days old).
- The Ravenna tourism site says "You cannot access the area with your car without a valid permit. Hotel guests can ask their host about a special access permit."
