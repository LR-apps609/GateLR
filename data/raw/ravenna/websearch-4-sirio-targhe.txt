- No source mentions a 48-hour deadline.
- Deadline: An older Rapida page says the documentation can be sent in advance or within five days after entering the ZTL, by fax or to sirio@comune.ra.it ("Invio della documentazione in modo preventivo, oppure entro cinque giorni successivi dall’accesso in ZTL"). That page is old.
- Hotel guests: The hotel must send the plate using the municipal form (Contrassegno tipo H - Strutture Ricettive Z.T.L.).
- Electric vehicles: A secondary guide says that hybrid or electric power does not grant access. No official Comune statement found.
- Disabled permit holders: "Modulo comunicazione targhe persone invalide", dated 29-04-2026; the permit holder must submit that form.
- Where to apply: Via D'Alaggio 3, or email permessi@comune.ravenna.it.
- Holders of ZTL permits already issued by the Polizia Municipale are exempt from the plate communication.
