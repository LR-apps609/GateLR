 Limited Traffic Zone (LTZ) 
 For access to Limited Traffic Zone (LTZ), all vehicles require special authorisations , certain categories of people or companies are entitled to permits that allow them to enter and park in these zones. 
 Go to permissions

 How to regularize unauthorized LTZ (Limited Traffic Zone) access : you can purchase the retrospectively activated transit ticket (ROZ) by 23.59 of the day after the day of access not previously authorised, at the rate of €15 . You can purchase it online from the reserved area go to the ZTL permits section and purchase the ROZ, or with the TapPark App go to the ZTL section, or at the Infomobility counter. Parking is not included. If you do not have an Italian tax code and are not yet registered, you can only register from the reserved area and then use the same credentials to access Tap&Park. At the time of purchase, please enter the date on which the event to be regularized occurred (not the date of payment). Click here to read the details on how to purchase the ROZ ticket online .

 All mopeds and motorbikes may travel in the ZTL with the exception of the Environmental and Pedestrian Islands . Only mopeds (not exceeding 50cc) and light quadricycles (not exceeding 50cc) and light quadricycles (registered as mopeds). Electric motorbikes and electric quadricycles heavy motorcycles (registered as motorbikes) to access the lanes must apply in writing as indicated here 
 Categories authorised to transit LTZ without a permit after accreditation 
 Rescue, police and emergency vehicles
 Tow truck for the removal of unauthorized vehicles in these areas
 Hearses in service
 Vehicles of authorised car sharing services in the city of Parma (ALLWAYS, Corrente)
 Vehicles used for the public transport service
 Vehicles for street cleaning and waste collection
 Operating means for the time strictly necessary for their use for repairs of infrastructures, networks or public service systems located there
 Vehicles used for the transport of valuables to banks located there
 Taxis and cars with NCC concession
 Vehicles of hotels customers located in the ZTL (exclusively for baggage transshipment operations)
 Vehicles identifiable by the particular color or by a specific logo in the name of the following companies and companies providing public utility services / Enia, BT Enia, Infomobility, Ad Personam, IRAIA, UniPR, Fondazione Teatro Regio, Teatro Regio, Azienda Ospedaliera, ARPAE, ACER, TEP, It-City, SMTP, AUSL, Poste Italiane, Municipality and Province of Parma, Iren, Ireti, Regione Emilia Romagna, Parma Infrastrutture, Ade, Aipo, Rai, Telecom Italia.
 Tommasini-Sauro Pedestrian Zone 
 Always on 24 hours 
 Only persons with the appropriate permit may enter: Residents, non-residents with parking space. Companies, businesses and artisans for maintenance work and business owners for loading and unloading goods: every day 9:00 - 10:30 / 15:00 - 15:30 (for access at other times it is necessary to apply for an exception permit from the local police) Vehicles for goods delivery with Ecologistics features: every day 7:00 - 10:30 / 15:00 - 15:30 Holders of ZTL parking permits may also access the environmental island. only with an authorization issued by the Local Police and Infomobility. 
 The access without permission, after accreditation, is possible only for: Rescue, police and emergency vehicles, Tow trucks for the removal of vehicles parked illegally in these areas, Hearses in service, Vehicles of authorised car sharing services in the city of Parma (ALLWAYS, Corrente), Vehicles used for scheduled public transport service, Vehicles for street cleaning and waste collection, Operating vehicles for the time strictly necessary for their use to repair infrastructure networks or installations of public services located there, Vehicles used to transport valuables to banks located there, Taxis and cars with NCC concession, Vehicles of hotel customers (exclusively for baggage transshipment operations), Vehicles identifiable by their particular colour or special logo registered to the following companies and companies providing public utility services. 
 Go to permissions 
 For commercial activities, with particular needs inherent to the activity itself, we invite you to submit a specific request to the Technical Parking Commission.
