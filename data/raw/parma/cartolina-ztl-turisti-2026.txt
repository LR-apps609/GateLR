Welcome to Parma!
Do you want to drive in Parma’s
Limited Traffic Zone (LTZ)?
Just purchase one of our access tickets
before entering:
• BZ – 2 hours for €5 (parking included!)
• GZ – Full day for €10 (parking included!)
You can buy them in several ways:
• Tap&Park app or online at www.infomobility.pr.it
• dedicated parking meters next to the electronic gates
• Infomobility desk at Viale Mentana 29A (see opening hours*)
• affiliated tobacconists and newsagents (see list*)
Entered the LTZ without authorization?
Don’t panic! You can still regularize your access by
purchasing a ROZ ticket (€15, parking not included) before
11:59 PM the following day.
How to regularize unauthorized LTZ (Limited Traffic Zone) access
• Tap&Park app or online at www.infomobility.pr.it
• Infomobility desk at Viale Mentana 29A (see opening hours*)
