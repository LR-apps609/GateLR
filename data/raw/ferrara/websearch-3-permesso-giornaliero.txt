The Comune di Ferrara's official pages say a single daily ZTL pass costs €20, and you can buy it online through the INES Cloud portal, with one important exception. These pages are roughly two years old.
- Price: The municipal daily-access page lists a single entry at €20 per day, and a 5- or 10-entry subscription as the other option. Payment at the counter (cash, debit card, credit card) or online through the personal area of the INES Cloud portal.
- Buying steps: After logging into your personal page on INES Cloud, choose PERMESSI ZTL, then PRENOTA/ACQUISTA; pay online.
- Exclusions: Online purchase is not available for the ZTL Duomo zone or for vehicles over 3.5 t. The ZTL Duomo is only open to people with a documented need, and that permit can only be requested at the counter.
- Timing: Daily passes must be bought before entering the zone.
- Payment caution: One municipal page warns not to pay through the Comune's PagoPA channel, and to pay only through the INES Cloud portal.
