ORDINANCE No. 77 of 11/05/2022

SUBJECT: NEW REGULATION OF MONTEPULCIANO “LIMITED TRAFFIC ZONE” (ZTL)



                                       THE COMMANDER

WHEREAS:
  • The Limited Traffic Zone was established in Montepulciano for the first time following a
    referendum held in 1989.
  • Since it was first established, the ZTL has undergone various changes, among other things
    by reason of continuous amendment to the law and regulations on the subject.
  • Most recently, in 2017, access control gates were set up, which allowed for better
    management of the ZTL, with positive effects on safety and liveability.
  • In previous years, the Municipal Council has passed its own resolutions implementing
    measures creating pedestrian areas in two sections of Via di Gracciano nel Corso.
  • Currently, the regulation of the Montepulciano ZTL is governed by Municipal Police
    Ordinances Nos. 1/2003 and 2/2003.
  • Various requests have been received by this Administration, from people living within the ZTL
    regarding the lack of parking areas, and from owners of restaurant and hospitality businesses
    regarding the redefinition of the hours for loading and unloading operations, for general
    purposes of improving the usability of the entire historic centre.
  • Among the various interventions, the reduction of the time when access would be allowed for
    loading and unloading of goods within the ZTL could produce further beneficial effects on
    traffic, deriving from the lesser presence of motor vehicles and positive effects in terms of
    safety, environmental protection, respect for the public right to peace and rest, as well as
    better enjoyment of the cultural, historical and artistic heritage, and the use in peace of
    neighbourhood shops, restaurant and hospitality businesses.
  • The Limited Traffic Zone is already an effective instrument, thanks to the use of telematics in
    the control of access.




Municipality of Montepulciano – Ordinance no. 77 of 11/05/2022
    GIVEN THAT:
    • One of the strategic objectives of the administration is to further raise the quality of life in the
       historical centre of Montepulciano, strengthening its role as a place of residence for families,
       as a natural commercial centre, as an institutional and cultural reference point for the region,
       and as a place for strolling and social gathering.
    • The ZTL, as it currently exists, has witnessed an increase in the flow of citizens in the
       morning, afternoon and evening hours, who come to this area for strolling and social
       gathering, as well as increasing frequency of tourist visitation.
    • The revision of the Montepulciano ZTL could give rise to further beneficial effects, deriving
       from reduced presence of motor vehicles and positive repercussions in terms of safety,
       environmental protection, better enjoyment of the cultural-historical and artistic heritage, and
       improved usability of the historic centre.

CONSIDERING IT APPROPRIATE, in implementation of the above, to proceed with the functional
adjustment of the regulations governing the Limited Traffic Zone in the Historic Centre of
Montepulciano, maintaining the presence of four (4) gates for selection and control over transit of
authorised vehicles (the gates “Porta al Prato”, with two video cameras, and “Collazzi”, “San Donato”
and “Fiorenzuola Vecchia”);
RECALLING the previous ordinances governing the Limited Traffic Zone of Montepulciano, namely
Ordinances Nos. 1/1998, 48/2003, 74/2006, 153/2006, 115/2009, 182/2009, 172/2010, 37/2011,
abrogated by subsequent references;
RESPECTING Ordinance No. 1/2013, the “Ordinance for the new regulation of the Montepulciano
limited traffic zone”;
RESPECTING Ordinance No. 2/2013, with object "Amendment of Ordinance No. 1 of 01/01/2013 in
the part concerning the regulation of pedestrian areas in the Historical Centre of Montepulciano”;
RESPECTING Ordinance No. 82/2016, with object "Establishment of control gates in the ZTL -
amendment and integration of Ordinance No. 1/2013, concerning the regulation of residents and
non-residents in the ZTL”;
RESPECTING Ordinance No. 55/2017, with object "Addition to Ordinance No. 1/2013";
CONSIDERING THE NECESSITY, therefore, to re-regulate traffic and vehicular circulation in the
Historic Centre of Montepulciano, for purposes of guaranteeing the safety of citizens and the
protection of health, urban safety, environmental and cultural heritage, and to allow for better
liveability within the Historic Centre;
CONSIDERING Article 3, paragraph 1, of Legislative Decree 285/1992, New Highway Code, which
states, in point 2), that the PEDESTRIAN AREA is the "area forbidden to the circulation of vehicles,
except for those in emergency service, bicycles and vehicles serving persons with limited or impeded
mobility, as well as possible exemptions for zero-emission vehicles with such dimensions and speed
as to be assimilated to bicycles. The municipalities may introduce, by means of appropriate
signalling, further restrictions on circulation in pedestrian areas”;
CONSIDERING Article 3, paragraph 1 of Legislative Decree 285/1992, New Highway Code, that
defines, at point no.54) the LIMITED TRAFFIC ZONE as the area in which vehicular access and
circulation are limited to predetermined hours or to particular categories of users and vehicles;
CONSIDERING paragraph 9 of Article 7 of Legislative Decree 285/1992, New Highway Code, which
provides that the Municipalities, by resolution of their Council, shall delimit the pedestrian areas and
the Limited Traffic Zones, taking into account the effects of vehicle circulation on traffic safety, health,
public order, environmental and cultural heritage, and on the environment;
CONSIDERING the Municipal Council Resolution No. 75 of 13/04/2022 concerning "New regulations
for the Limited Traffic Zone (ZTL) of Montepulciano";

Municipality of Montepulciano – Ordinance no. 77 of 11/05/2022
                                              ORDERS
That, beginning 01/06/2022, the organisation of the Limited Traffic Zone (ZTL) of Montepulciano is
regulated as follows.
That the entire area of the historic centre of Montepulciano is defined, for the purposes of mobility,
as a Limited Traffic Zone (ZTL).
That within this area, vehicular access, circulation and parking are regulated by this ordinance.

That vehicular transit is prohibited for:
    • vehicles with a total mass exceeding 7.5 tonnes;
    • motor caravans;
    • buses, coaches;
Vehicular transit is prohibited, beginning at Porta a Prato, for vehicles over 3.50 metres in height;
Vehicular transit is prohibited, beginning from Via di Ciliano, for vehicles over 2.50 metres in height;
Vehicular transit is prohibited, beginning from Via di Collazzi, for vehicles over 2.70 metres in height;
Vehicular transit is prohibited, beginning from Via di San Donato, for vehicles over 3.70 metres in
height.

That the Limited Traffic Zone (ZTL) of Montepulciano consists of the following Zones, better shown
in the attached plan (Annex A), which is an integral part of this Ordinance:

The Limited Traffic Zone is effective every day (weekdays and holidays) in the following
hours:
   • 1 May to 30 September, for 24 hours per day;
   • In the months of April and October, from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.;
   • the rest of the year, from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

In the above hours, vehicle entrance and transit in the ZTL are forbidden. No exemptions are
provided for vehicles with electric and/or hybrid engines.

Entrance and transit for operations of loading and unloading goods are permitted within the
ZTL on working days and holidays (365 days per year), only from 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m.

Within the ZTL, parking by unauthorised vehicles is forbidden, 24 hours per day.

Within the ZTL, parking is permitted solely for vehicles in possession of the "Residents" badge or
similar.
Unauthorised vehicles parked in the parking areas reserved for residents will be subject to forced
removal.

This is without prejudice to the specific limitations on Urban Pedestrian Areas within the ZTL, which
are regulated by a relative ordinance.

Circulation and parking in derogation of the prohibitions arising from this ordinance are established
by the provisions laid down for each category listed below.

Circulation and parking will be authorised within the Limited Traffic Zone, where permitted,
for vehicles pertaining to:

   1. RESIDENTS:
      The members of each family registered as resident within the limited traffic zone may be
      authorised to transit and park.
      The authorisation allows circulation and parking in the car parking areas, within the family’s
      own Zone of residence.
      The following types of vehicles may be authorised: motor vehicles for transport of persons,
      of which the owner, or user (for leased vehicles) or the holder of a long-term rental contract
      is resident within the ZTL.
      The authorisation is valid for one year, and if no changes are communicated by the holder,
      is automatically renewed on 31 December of each calendar year.

   2. OWNERS OF PROPERTY (civil dwelling or similar) which is not rented to third parties:
      Owners of property located within the Limited Traffic Zone, but who are not residents in the
      Limited Traffic Zone, may be issued a single authorisation limited solely to transit.
      The authorisation allows parking in the areas designated for loading/unloading operations
      and in spaces reserved for resident parking for a maximum of 20 minutes, with display of
      their time of arrival.
      If the property is in the name of several individuals, any additional authorisations to the co-
      owners will be issued solely for transit and loading/unloading. The types of vehicles
      authorised are the same as those indicated for residents.

       The authorisation is valid for one year, and if no changes are communicated by the holder is
       automatically renewed on 31 December of each calendar year.

   3. TENANTS (non-residents):
      Persons who are tenants of property zoned as a civil dwelling and/or similar - provided these
      hold a regularly registered rental contract - may be issued a single authorisation allowing
      circulation within the area where the property is located.
      The authorisation allows parking in the areas designated for loading/unloading operations
      and in spaces reserved for resident parking for a maximum of 20 minutes, with display of
      time of arrival.
      The validity of the authorisation will be commensurate with the duration of the rental contract.

   4. HOME CARE ASSISTANTS
      Persons engaged in the provision of home care to non-self-sufficient persons residing in the
      ZTL may be issued with a single authorisation allowing transit only in the destination zone,
      and parking in the spaces reserved for residents for a maximum of 2 hours, with display of
      the arrival time.
      The authorisation is issued upon presentation of an application accompanied by medical
      certification attesting to the family member’s need for assistance.
      The validity of the authorisation is commensurate with the medical certification presented or
      the need that led to its issue, and in any case will be for a maximum of 1 year.
      The authorisations are renewable subject to proof of continuation of the reasons for the
      previous issue.

   5. HEALTH AND WELFARE SERVICES:
      Social cooperatives, not-for-profit organisations (ONLUS) and other agencies that provide
      services for payment or under an agreement with public agencies or Local Health Units (USL)
      may be issued with an authorisation allowing circulation in all zones, and parking for a
      maximum of 60 minutes in the spaces reserved for residents, with display of arrival time.

   6. ACCOMPANIMENT OF SCHOOL-AGE MINORS
      Persons accompanying or picking up minors at the "Fausto Fumi" nursery school (located
      within the ZTL) may be issued a single authorisation consisting of a paper badge,
      associated with a maximum of two license plates of authorised vehicles.
      The authorisation allows transit only in zone B, where the school is located, along the shortest
      route, i.e.: Via di Collazzi, Via Piana, Piazza Santa Lucia, Via del Macellino, Via Borgo Buio,
      Via di Gozzano.
      Parking is permitted near the nursery school in the spaces provided for residents, for a
      maximum of 10 minutes and with display of arrival time.
      The authorisation will be valid for the duration of the school year and limited to the times of
      school entrance and exit.

   7. CONSTRUCTION ACTIVITIES:
      A temporary authorisation may be issued to owners of companies carrying out maintenance,
      repair, installation and/or construction work on building sites within the ZTL. A permit is issued
      for each authorised vehicle. The authorisation allows transit in the area where the
      construction site is located.
      In the event that vehicle parking cannot be carried out on the area of the construction site,
      parking may be authorised in the residents’ spaces in the vicinity of the construction site and
      where not expressly prohibited, in compliance with the provisions of Article 158 of the
      Highway Code.
      If the worksite is located in one of the Pedestrian Areas, then the authorisation may also allow
      access to that area, with a short stop limited to the time strictly necessary for loading and
      unloading materials and work equipment. The authorisation shall be valid for a maximum of
      one year.

      Owners of companies carrying out urgent maintenance, repair, installation, trade work and/or

      At the same time as the authorisation (subject to payment of the amount due), a numbered

      reason for each entry outside the loading and unloading hours.
      The applicant must prove, by means of Chamber of Commerce Certification, the performance
      of one (or more) of the following services or other related activities:
          • Systems: electrical, plumbing, heating, refrigeration, lifts, security and alarm,

          • Cleaning services: cleaning and sanitation, pest or rodent control;
          • Trade work: small construction and maintenance works, carpentry, glaziers,
               metalworkers, upholsterers etc.
      Transit is permitted in the entire ZTL and without restrictions on hours, along the shortest
      route reaching the workplace.
      Parking is permitted in residents’ spaces near the workplace and in compliance with the
      provisions of Article 158 of the Highway Code, with display of a special sign with the wording
      "TRADES - URGENT REPAIRS".
      At the request of the Traffic Police, the person concerned must show the self-certification
      indicating the address and reason for the work being performed.
      A badge is issued for each authorised vehicle.
      The authorisation is valid for one year and, if no changes are communicated by the company
      holding the authorisation, is automatically renewed on 31 December of each calendar year.

   9. PRIVATE SECURITY AND VALUABLES TRANSPORT COMPANIES:
      The authorisation consists of a badge and registration on the "White List".
      One badge is issued for each authorised vehicle.
      The authorisation is valid for a maximum of one year.
      The authorisation allows transit in all zones, without time restrictions.
      Vehicles equipped for the transport of valuables are allowed to transit through pedestrian
      areas.
      Stopping is permitted - without parking - provided the vehicle does not cause an obstruction.
      The applicant company must present a Chamber of Commerce certification demonstrating
      the business activity of private security or transport of valuables.
      Vehicles registered in the company's name with a gross vehicle weight of less than 3.5 tons
      are permitted.

   10. DOCTORS AND OTHER MEDICAL PERSONNEL:
       Doctors affiliated with the National Health Service (SSN) or Health Service of the Region of
       Tuscany (SSRT), who have their clinic or patients within the ZTL, and other medical
       personnel who carry out home visits/services in the ZTL (obstetricians, nurses,
       physiotherapists, orthopaedic specialists) may be provided with authorisation, consisting of
       a single badge bearing a maximum of two vehicle license numbers. The authorisation allows
       transit in all zones without time restrictions and parking in residents’ spaces.

   11. SHORT-TERM ACCOMODATIONS:
       Accommodation facilities operating with VAT numbers and situated within the ZTL may obtain
       authorisation for temporary transit of guest vehicles within the ZTL, for purposes of loading
       and unloading baggage.
       The management of the accommodations will ensure conformity with municipal regulations
       by entering the guest’s vehicle license number in the temporary "White List", using the relative
       Internet service.
       In entering the guest’s license number on the White List, the management must indicate the
       period of stay.
       Transit is permitted only by the shortest route to the accommodations.
       Short-term guests are never permitted to park within the ZTL.
       Stopping is permitted for a maximum of 20 minutes, in spaces reserved for loading and
       unloading or where otherwise permitted in the immediate vicinity of the accommodation, with
       display of the badge issued by the facility itself, and with display of the time of arrival.
       In each White List entry, management must enter the record number of the relative
       “Generalities of Guests”, pursuant to Article 109 of the Public Safety Code.
       No authorisation is ever issued for vehicles registered in the name of the accommodation
       facility personnel.

   12. SALES AGENTS AND REPRESENTATIVES; AGENTS FOR ARTICLES OF VALUE:
       Representatives, commercial agents and personnel of similar status, with voluminous, heavy
       or valuable samples, who need to access the ZTL for their work, may be issued with a single
       authorisation, consisting of a badge bearing only one vehicle license number, with insertion
       of the license number on the temporary “White List”.
       Representatives carrying valuables may be issued with an authorisation in derogation of the
       loading and unloading hours.
       The authorisation allows transit in all zones and parking in residents’ spaces for a maximum
       of 1 hour, with display of arrival time.
       The applicant for authorisation must present a Chamber of Commerce certification
       demonstrating their status as representative, commercial agent or agent for valuables.
       For employees acting as agent on behalf of a company, the application must include the
       Chamber of Commerce certification of the company and the company certification of the
       employee's position.

   13. TRANSPORT OF MEDICINES, PHARMACEUTICAL PRODUCTS:
       Companies that transport medicines or pharmaceutical products may be authorised to transit
       in the ZTL.
       The authorisation allows transit in all zones without time restrictions, for the time strictly
       necessary to carry out loading and unloading operations.
       Transit is permitted according to the shortest route to the pharmacy or relative destination.

       In the case of use of a vehicle other than the one associated with the badge, the interested
       parties must, on each occasion, request the inclusion of the relative number plate in the White

   15. PERSONS WITH TEMPORARY IMPAIRMENT TO MOBILITY:
       Authorisation may be issued to persons with temporary impairment to mobility and who need
       to enter the ZTL for treatment, work or other needs. The relative permit is limited to
       authorisation of transit within the specific zone, as well as parking in residents’ spaces for a
       maximum of 2 hours, with display of arrival time, except in the case of specific and proven
       needs.
       The authorisation is issued upon presentation of a specific application, accompanied by
       medical certification.
       The validity of the authorisation shall be commensurate with the medical certification
       submitted, and in any case for a maximum of six months.
       The authorisation is renewable subject to proof of the continuation of the reasons as originally
       cited.

   16. PREGNANT WOMEN; FAMILIES WITH CHILDREN AGED LESS THAN TWO YEARS:
       Pregnant women who are registered residents in the Municipality of Montepulciano may be
       issued a single-vehicle authorisation allowing circulation in the ZTL, and also parking in
       resident spaces without time restrictions.
       Such temporary authorisation is issued to the pregnant woman, and also after the birth of the
       child, to a person exercising parental responsibility or to a person they delegate.
       The permit is issued following the submission of an application, accompanied by a medical
       certificate from the Local Health Unit (ASL) certifying the state of pregnancy with an indication
       of the expected date of delivery, or a declaration stating the birth and residence of the child.
       The permit is valid until the second birthday of the child, or if requested during pregnancy,
       until two years after the expected date of birth.

   17. POLICE AND EMERGENCY RESPONDERS:

       The authorisation allows transit in all zones without time constraints, and parking for a
       maximum of two hours in places assigned for residents, with display of arrival time.
       For lorries or operating vehicles engaged in repair work, parking is permitted near the
       worksite, provided there is no hindrance to traffic.
