- Effective date and legal basis: The changes took effect on 1 August 2025, under Giunta resolution n. 280 of 10 July 2025 and dirigential ordinances n. 1679 and n. 1689 (comune.lecce.it).
- Weekday access: Electronic gates are off during 10:00–13:00 and 16:30–20:00 (ecodallecitta). Outside those windows, only authorized vehicles can enter (autovia.it).
- Sundays and holidays: Sources differ on wording. One says access is barred for the whole day (ecodallecitta), and another describes the ZTL as active 24 hours a day (autovia.it).
- Porta Napoli: Transit through this gate is prohibited at all times, with the ZTL described as active 24 hours there (leccesette.it).
- Loading and unloading: Weekdays allow 5:30–9:30 and 15:00–16:00. Sundays and holidays allow 6:00–9:30 (comune.lecce.it PDF, Ordinanza 1689).
- Old permits: Existing C/S and LOG permits stayed valid until 31 December 2025 (telepass moveo).
- Conflicting pre-decision report: A Quotidiano di Puglia article described different proposed hours (gates open 10:30–13:30 and 16:30–20:30). That was a pre-decision proposal, not the final rule.
