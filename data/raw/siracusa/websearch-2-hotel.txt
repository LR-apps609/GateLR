- Current platform: The tourism site says the new Connect Pass platform has been officially active on an experimental basis since Thursday 11 June 2026. It also states that all earlier channels (Simple Pass, paper forms, Google forms, Sirio Web and Sirio Pass) have been permanently disabled.
- Deadline: The municipality's ZTL page says operators must send the vehicle plate and length of stay to the Police Command within 48 hours of the guests' transit.
- Access only, no parking: Accommodation operators can authorize guests' vehicles for transit only; parking inside the ZTL is prohibited, and the pass is issued only for loading and unloading luggage. The temporary permits can cover only access to the ZTL, not parking.
- Daily limit: The maximum number of daily passes an operator can generate depends on the number of rooms registered and recorded for that property.
- Residents' groups have criticized the system. One committee reported that only 540 spaces are available in the ZTL, yet over 8,600 passes have already been issued.
