- Current Ortigia ZTL hours (from 1 April 2026): a new Mobility and Transport ordinance took effect on 1 April 2026, replacing the winter schedule. Monday to Saturday, access to the island is banned from 11:00 to 15:30 and from 17:00 to 02:00 the next day. On Sundays and holidays, the ban starts at 10:00 and runs without a break until 02:00 the next day. Residents of Ortigia and authorized vehicles are exempt. The Comune's own ZTL page lists the same weekday and Sunday windows.
- Existence and date: The Comune di Siracusa's ZTL page lists ordinance n. 189 dated 30/03/2026, alongside n. 417 of 2025 and n. 292 of 08/05/2026. The 292 ordinance may have modified the rules, and I could not check its content.
- Loading and unloading: Goods deliveries are allowed only outside ZTL hours, from 02:00 to 11:00 and from 15:30 to 17:00. Vehicles must park in the designated bays for up to 30 minutes with a parking disc, and cannot enter pedestrian areas. Loading is always prohibited on Sundays and holidays. (A May 2026 Siracusa Press article: a councillor called for changes; later headline "ZTL Ortigia, cambia il regolamento: consegna merci anche nei festivi".)
- Winter 2025–26 schedule: Ordinance 791 of 23/12/2025 set reduced hours from 24 December 2025 to 31 March 2026. Friday and pre-holiday days ran 20:00 to 24:00, Saturday 17:00 to 24:00, and Sunday and holidays 11:00 to 24:00. It has expired.
- Validity period: I found no source stating an expiry date for ordinance 189. One site says a lighter winter regime is re-issued each year, but it gives no dates for that regime.
