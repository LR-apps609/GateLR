HOW TO ACCESS BERGAMO CITTÀ ALTA
THE LIMITED TRAFFIC ZONES (ZTL) OF CITTÀ ALTA FOR CARS WITHOUT SPECIFIC PERMITS
        ACCESS FOR PARKING – CITTÀ ALTA AND COLLI LIMITED TRAFFIC ZONES
  Access to Città Alta for parking is always permitted, 7 days a week, 24 hours a day, even when the Limited
  Traffic Zone /ZTL) is active, but only if you enter from Viale Vittorio Emanuele II, at the Galleria Conca d’Oro
  roundabout, with the obligation to park within 30 minutes of entry in one of the following car parks:
      Città Alta Car Park – Via della Fara
      Tre Armi Car Park – Via Tre Armi
      S. Alessandro Car Park – Largo Porta S. Alessandro
  The signs at the Galleria Conca d’Oro roundabout display parking availability in real time.
