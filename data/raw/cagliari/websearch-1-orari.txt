- Marina: 21:00–07:00 and 15:30–17:00 (every day).
- Stampace alto: 21:00–09:00 and 15:30–17:00 (one source says 21:00 to 10:00).
- Stampace basso: weekdays 07:30–10:30 and 15:30–17:00; Saturday 07:30–10:30 (sources disagree on Saturdays).
- Villanova: 21:00–07:00 and 15:30–17:00 (every day).
- Poetto: 00:00–24:00 per the archived page; another source says every day from 21.00 to 6.00.
Comunicati del Comune (2016, 2018): fascia ZTL di Castello e Stampace alto estesa per eventi (varchi aperti ai non autorizzati dalle 20 alle 24; ZTL Castello estesa 9-20 per Monumenti Aperti).
Unione Sarda (luglio 2025): cameras switched back on, about 13,920 violations detected in one year; new rules end free permit renewals and add temporary passes, plus penalties for overstaying the exit time.
tyremap.com: fine roughly €80–335, the exact amount applied by the city is not confirmed on the official portal; says the weekday 7:30–19:30 schedule circulating online is wrong.
