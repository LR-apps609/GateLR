- Hours: Most sources say the central Modena ZTL has no free time slot. One guide says the ZTL is active every day, 24 hours a day, with no free time band. Another says the ZTL is usually continuous, often 24 hours, though some areas use wide bands such as 7:30 to 20:30 on weekdays and pre-holidays. The Comune's own ordinance excerpt lists separate bands for specific streets, such as Via Emilia Centro and Corso Duomo, and does not give a single citywide schedule.
- Cameras: Entry points are watched by cameras running 24 hours a day, which record plates and send them to an automated check against authorized vehicles. One source puts the fine at 83 to 332 euro under article 7 of the Highway Code, with no points deducted.
- Tourist access: A daily permit is issued by the Polizia Municipale, with one source citing a cost of 4 euro (source about 867 days old). Another source says temporary permits are requested online through the portal.
- Late notice: The Comune's ordinance says that for entry between 20:00 and 06:00, notice must reach the Polizia Municipale before or within 48 hours after access, including the passenger's details and vehicle identifiers.
- One source notes the Comune's ZTL page was last updated on 13 March 2025.
