- Online portal: The Comune di Matera says plate communications from lodging facilities go through its online portal, where the facility creates a profile and submits a new request. The portal is reached at matera.sismic.it, and the lodging operator then clicks the RICHIESTA PASS link. The portal for lodging facilities covers only the ZTLs where guests may be allowed in for luggage loading and unloading, plus car parks.
- Temporary permits: An older Comune description says guests of hotels, guesthouses and tourist accommodation inside the Matera ZTL receive a temporary permit for access to the property. That permit covers the time strictly needed for check-in and for loading and unloading luggage.
- A tourism site adds that most lodgings in the Sassi arrange a transit permit for luggage drop-off, requested on arrival.
- Electric cars: I found no evidence that Matera grants electric cars the same access.
- None of the results say how Matera treats motorcycles or mopeds.
- Fleet Magazine: Matera ha ZTL e APU (aree con varchi e/o dissuasori) con controllo elettronico: entrare senza autorizzazione può portare sanzioni.
