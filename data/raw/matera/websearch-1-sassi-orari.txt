- Latest found (Ord. 147/2025): The gates are active from 10:00 to 24:00 in summer and from 10:00 to 20:30 in winter. Periodo estivo compreso dal 01 luglio al 15 settembre, di ogni anno, dalle ore 10.00 alle ore 24:00; periodo invernale compreso dal 16 settembre al 30 giugno, di ogni anno, dalle ore 10.00 alle ore 20:30. The same sheet of hours lists the Sassi gates as 10:00–20:30 from 16/09 to 30/06 (ZTL ATTIVA: ACCESSO NON CONSENTITO).
- The Comune's timetable lists it alongside 148/2025 as covering ZTL Piave hours and changes to the activation hours of the Rioni Sassi ZTL. N° 147 e 148/2025 (Orari ZTL Piave e Modifica orari di attivazione ZTL Rioni Sassi) The Sassi gates on Via Buozzi, Via Madonna delle Virtù and Via D'Addozio are listed with two bands, 07:00–10:30 and 15:00–17:00 (loading).
- Older and conflicting sources: Ord. 473/2023 set the gates to run dal lunedì alla domenica dalle ore 10.00 alle ore 20:30.
- A 2026 Confesercenti release says the gates were opened on a trial basis from 13:00 to 15:30 between 19 January and 15 March 2026, so restaurant customers could reach the Sassi at lunchtime. That trial has already ended.
- Summer 2026: I found no ordinance changing the ZTL for June–October 2026.
- Third-party sites: ztlitaly.com and tyremap.com give conflicting hours (one says daily 10:00–24:00, the other 09:00–20:00).
