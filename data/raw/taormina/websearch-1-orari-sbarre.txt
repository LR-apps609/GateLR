- accessibilitacentristorici.it (https://www.accessibilitacentristorici.it/ztl/sicilia/taormina.html): the ZTL of Taormina is in operation 24 hours a day; currently the cameras are not in operation. Loading and unloading for goods in all weekdays, excluding Sundays and holidays, from 06.00 to 09.30. Gates indicated: via Dionisio, Corso Umberto I at Porta Catania, via Cappuccini.

- qds.it (data non visibile): the bollards were meant to protect pedestrians on Corso Umberto; loading and unloading was allowed only from 5 to 9 in the morning.

- bluekeys.it (guida 2026): restriction every day 10.30–13.00 and 16.00–20.00, with an extra summer window 21.00–01.00; the source itself says this is not a municipal act.
- tyremap.com: hotel guests exempt after registering the plate, fines roughly 83–160 euro; the "24/7 in summer" claim is not officially confirmed.
