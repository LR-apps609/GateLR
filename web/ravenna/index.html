ZTL · entry with a day ticket

Ravenna · Historic centre ZTL

The historic centre (Piazza del Popolo, San Vitale) is watched by SIRIO cameras: most gates are on 24 hours a day, three only 7:30–20:30. Without a permit, buy a day permit (15 €, or 8 € for the morning or afternoon) at the grey parking meters before entering. If your hotel is inside, it registers your plate.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Day ticket

€15.00

Activate it before you drive in. Day permit 15 € (0:00–23:59), or 8 € for the morning (0:00–11:59) or afternoon (12:00–23:59), from the grey «titanium» parking meters, 24 hours a day, for vehicles up to 5 t: coins (no change) or credit card. Buy it before you pass the camera and display it on the dashboard.

How to buy it ↗

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

Display it on the dashboard; it allows parking in ZTL streets for up to 90 minutes with a parking disc, never in Piazza del Popolo or pedestrian areas.

  • Electric and hybrid cars are not exempt (no exemption in the City's pages).
If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Ravenna ZTL active?

Ravenna: The historic centre (Piazza del Popolo, San Vitale) is watched by SIRIO cameras: most gates are on 24 hours a day, three only 7:30–20:30. Without a permit, buy a day permit (15 €, or 8 € for the morning or afternoon) at the grey parking meters before entering. If your hotel is inside, it registers your plate.

How much is the fine for entering the Ravenna ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Ravenna ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Ravenna). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
