ZTL · no entry without a permit

ZTL · Historic Centre

Closed without a permit Monday to Friday 6:30 am–6 pm and Saturday 2–6 pm, and on Friday and Saturday nights 11 pm–3 am (not in August). Public holidays are free. There is no day ticket for visitors, electric cars included. If you sleep in a hotel inside the ZTL and the hotel is signed up to the city's service, it can register your plate by the same day you drive in.

When it's on

Mon – Fri6:30 am – 6 pm
Sat2 pm – 6 pm
Fri, Sat nights (1 Jan – 31 Jul)11 pm – 3 am
Fri, Sat nights (1 Sep – 31 Dec)11 pm – 3 am
Sundayopen to all

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

How much is the fine for entering the Rome ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Rome ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (ZTL and ZTL). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
