ZTL · entry with a day ticket

Centro Storico · ZTL Centrale

Mon – Fri, 8 am – 8 pmNot reviewed yet
ZTL

The old centre (from the station to via Cavour, from Porta Nuova to the sea) is closed without a pass Monday to Friday 8 am–8 pm, and on Friday and Saturday nights until 6 am: from 8 pm in May–October, from 11 pm in November–April. Sundays and public holidays have no daytime restriction. Anyone can drive in with a day pass (€5; €2.50 hybrids and LPG/methane; free for registered electric cars) if the car is at least petrol Euro 3 or diesel Euro 4. Motorbikes are free. Via Maqueda and the Cassaro (corso Vittorio Emanuele) are pedestrian areas: no cars even with a pass. If your hotel is inside, it can have your plate registered for arrival and departure.

Day ticket

€5.00

Activate it before you drive in. Buy it online on the city's ZTL portal or in the PalerMobilità app, at AMAT offices (e.g. Stazione Centrale, Politeama, Piazzale Ungheria car park) or authorised resellers, then activate it with your plate before you drive in (app, portal or SMS to 339 9942927 with «ZTL plate code»; with a foreign number use the app or portal). €2.50 for hybrids and LPG/methane cars. Not for petrol Euro 0–2 or diesel Euro 0–3.

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

Motorbikes and scooters: always free.

Still being checked
  • Perimeter from the city's official map (Municipal Police KML «ZTL_2019», still the map on its «Info e Mappa» page). GateLR uses its main outline; about 24 small blocks just outside it (along the southern edge near Corso Tukory, near Porta Nuova and near via Cavour) are also marked ZTL on that map and are not shown. The perimeter streets themselves are free. In February 2026 the city (Giunta resolution 22/2026) extended the free-transit streets (e.g. around piazza Verdi and via Cadorna–via del Bastione) and moved some gates: the official map does not show this yet.
  • Gate cameras: the city doesn't publish their positions, so GateLR shows none. Crossing live gates several times within 15 minutes counts as one entry.
  • Public holidays: AMAT says there are no restrictions on Sundays and holidays, and the official map says «working days». Neither says whether the Friday/Saturday night band applies when that evening is a holiday; GateLR treats a night that starts on a holiday as free.
  • 15 July (Santa Rosalia, patron saint): GateLR does not count it as a holiday, because the city switches the ZTL off with a separate order each year (14–15 July in 2023, 2024 and 2025; in 2026 until 8 am on 16 July). It does the same for Holy Week and Christmas (22 December–1 January in 2025/26): check AMAT's news before you drive in.
  • Day-pass deadline: AMAT and the city portal say to activate the pass before entering, and the city's service page says it can't be used for an earlier date. The 2024 rules (and the police page) allow activation afterwards: Monday–Thursday by midnight of the day of entry, Friday–Sunday nights by 6 am. A pass for the Saturday-night band must be activated after 6 am on Saturday. With a foreign mobile number use the PalerMobilità app or the web portal, not SMS. GateLR says: before you enter.
  • Engines: petrol Euro 0–2 and diesel Euro 0–3 cars can't get any pass (only residents may enter with them). Hybrids and petrol/LPG or petrol/methane cars pay €2.50 a day whatever their Euro class (GateLR checks them like petrol cars). Fully electric cars: free pass, but the car must first be registered with AMAT; without it, treat it like any other car. Motorbikes and mopeds may enter freely.
  • Hotel guests (hotels, B&Bs and holiday homes inside the ZTL): the property asks AMAT to put your plate on the white list, even afterwards (Monday–Thursday by midnight of the day, Friday–Sunday nights by 6 am). The first entry to unload luggage and the final exit are free; for the days in between you need a pass (hotel-guest rates: €5 a day, €15 a week, €30 a month). The number of plates is limited to the property's capacity; show your booking if stopped.
  • Disabled badge holders (CUDE) from outside Palermo: e-mail the badge and the car's registration to ztlcentrale@amat.pa.it before or within 15 days of the transit, or register on the ZTL portal.
  • Since May 2026 the pass can also be paid automatically through the Telepass app (service active from midnight after you switch it on).
  • Not on the map: Mondello's summer evening ZTL by the beach (8 May–30 September 2026, 8 pm–midnight, until 1 am on Saturdays and the eves of holidays; set again each year), the evening pedestrian area of via del Celso–Salita Castellana (8 pm–1 am) and many small pedestrian streets inside the centre (Vucciria, Ballarò, piazza Caracciolo and others, made permanent in 2026).

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Palermo ZTL active?

Centro Storico: The old centre (from the station to via Cavour, from Porta Nuova to the sea) is closed without a pass Monday to Friday 8 am–8 pm, and on Friday and Saturday nights until 6 am: from 8 pm in May–October, from 11 pm in November–April. Sundays and public holidays have no daytime restriction. Anyone can drive in with a day pass (€5; €2.50 hybrids and LPG/methane; free for registered electric cars) if the car is at least petrol Euro 3 or diesel Euro 4. Motorbikes are free. Via Maqueda and the Cassaro (corso Vittorio Emanuele) are pedestrian areas: no cars even with a pass. If your hotel is inside, it can have your plate registered for arrival and departure.

How much is the fine for entering the Palermo ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Palermo ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Centro Storico). Write to the hotel before you arrive.

Is there a day ticket for the Palermo ZTL?

Centro Storico: €5.00, activate it before you drive in. Buy it online on the city's ZTL portal or in the PalerMobilità app, at AMAT offices (e.g. Stazione Centrale, Politeama, Piazzale Ungheria car park) or authorised resellers, then activate it with your plate before you drive in (app, portal or SMS to 339 9942927 with «ZTL plate code»; with a foreign number use the app or portal). €2.50 for hybrids and LPG/methane cars. Not for petrol Euro 0–2 or diesel Euro 0–3.

Can I drive into the pedestrian areas of Palermo?

No. Cars can't drive into pedestrian areas at any time, not even with a permit or a ticket. Park outside and walk.
