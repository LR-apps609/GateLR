Old town · ZTL from via Roma to via Duomo

⚠ Unofficial source: always check the sign at the gate

Salerno's historic centre (between via Roma and via Duomo / via San Benedetto, plus Corso Vittorio Emanuele) is a camera-controlled ZTL with 14 gates, such as Portanova, via Duomo and via dei Principati, reportedly active 24 hours a day. Without a permit stay out. Staying inside? Your hotel or B&B sends your transit to the City's ZTL office: ask them before you arrive.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Salerno ZTL active?

Old town: Salerno's historic centre (between via Roma and via Duomo / via San Benedetto, plus Corso Vittorio Emanuele) is a camera-controlled ZTL with 14 gates, such as Portanova, via Duomo and via dei Principati, reportedly active 24 hours a day. Without a permit stay out. Staying inside? Your hotel or B&B sends your transit to the City's ZTL office: ask them before you arrive.

How much is the fine for entering the Salerno ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Salerno ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Old town). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
