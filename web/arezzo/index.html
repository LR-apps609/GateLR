ZTL · no entry without a permit

Corso Italia · ZTL B and Piazza Grande

Closed to unauthorised vehicles every day, holidays included, from 8 am to midnight; open from midnight to 8 am. Motorbikes need a permit too. Hotel guests with a booking may drive to the hotel only to unload luggage: you or the hotel must enter the plate on the city's ZTL portal. There is no tourist ticket: the paid day pass (only for real needs such as loading) allows 8–11 am and 2–4 pm, stopping 20 minutes at most. Piazza Grande and the other pedestrian squares are closed to cars at all times.

When it's on

Mon – Sun8 am – 12 am

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

“Nella ZTL B vige il divieto di circolazione e sosta per i veicoli a motore (sprovvisti di regolare autorizzazione) tutti i giorni dalle ore 8,00 alle ore 24,00.”

In ZTL B, driving and parking are forbidden to motor vehicles (without a valid authorisation) every day from 8:00 to 24:00.

ZTL · no entry without a permit

Centro · Historic Centre ZTL A

Monday to Saturday (not on public holidays), 8:30 am–12 noon and 5–8 pm: no entry without a permit, and cameras also check the way out, so leave outside those hours too. Open at all other times and all day on Sundays and holidays. Motorbikes and mopeds may enter. Hotel guests with a booking may drive to the hotel only to unload luggage: you or the hotel must enter the plate on the city's ZTL portal. There is no tourist ticket: the paid day pass is for real needs such as loading (8:30 am–12 noon). The core around Corso Italia and Piazza Grande is ZTL B, closed 8 am–midnight every day.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

How much is the fine for entering the Arezzo ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Arezzo ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Corso Italia, Lower Corso Italia, Centro and Via Marconi). Write to the hotel before you arrive.

Can I drive into the pedestrian areas of Arezzo?

No. Cars can't drive into pedestrian areas at any time, not even with a permit or a ticket. Park outside and walk.
