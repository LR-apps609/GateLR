ZTL · no entry without a permit

Walled city · ZTL Centro Storico

The whole walled old town of Alghero (inside the bastions, from Porta a Mare and Porta Terra to Piazza Sulis) is closed to vehicles without a permit all year, every day: midnight–8 am, 10:30 am–2:30 pm and 4:30 pm–midnight. Open to all only 8–10:30 am and 2:30–4:30 pm. Cameras at Via Simon/Piazza Porta Terra and Piazza Sulis. Staying inside? Your hotel gives your plate to the Local Police, only to unload luggage. Park outside the walls and walk.

When it's on

Mon – Sun12 am – 8 am
Mon – Sun10:30 am – 2:30 pm
Mon – Sun4:30 pm – 12 am

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

Official source

City of Alghero · Official page ↗

“Tutta l’area del centro storico cittadino come sopra perimetrata è connotata come Zona a Traffico Limitato con conseguente divieto di circolazione e sosta per tutte le categorie di veicoli fatte salve le deroghe esplicitamente previste – tutto l’anno - dalle ore 00:00 alle ore 08:00, dalle ore 10:30 alle ore 14:30 e dalle ore 16:30 alle ore 24:00”

The whole area of the historic town centre, bounded as above, is a Limited Traffic Zone, with a consequent ban on driving and parking for all categories of vehicles, except for the exemptions expressly provided, all year round, from 00:00 to 08:00, from 10:30 to 14:30 and from 16:30 to 24:00.

  • Hotels and B&Bs inside the walls tell the Local Police the plate of the car you use to reach them: you may then drive in only to load and unload luggage, not to park. Ask your hotel before you arrive.
  • Fully electric cars may enter, but only after their plate has been registered in writing on the City's white list. Blue Badge holders not resident in Alghero who enter in an emergency must send the Local Police the plate, date, time and a copy of the badge within 72 hours.

Some unofficial guides give different summer evening hours. The ZTL is also closed to vehicles over 2.5 t; pedestrian areas inside have their own rules.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Alghero ZTL active?

Walled city: The whole walled old town of Alghero (inside the bastions, from Porta a Mare and Porta Terra to Piazza Sulis) is closed to vehicles without a permit all year, every day: midnight–8 am, 10:30 am–2:30 pm and 4:30 pm–midnight. Open to all only 8–10:30 am and 2:30–4:30 pm. Cameras at Via Simon/Piazza Porta Terra and Piazza Sulis. Staying inside? Your hotel gives your plate to the Local Police, only to unload luggage. Park outside the walls and walk.

How much is the fine for entering the Alghero ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Alghero ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Walled city). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
