ZTL · entry with a day ticket

ZTL 1 · Duomo, Pilotta and Via Garibaldi

Mon – Sun, 7:30 am – 7:30 pmNot reviewed yet
ZTL

Every day from 7:30 am to 7:30 pm, holidays included, you can't drive in without a permit or a ticket: GZ day ticket €10 or BZ €5 for 2 hours, bought and activated before the camera (parking included). Forgot? Buy a €15 ROZ by 23:59 the next day. Motorbikes may enter; electric cars may not. If your hotel is inside, it registers your plate by the day you arrive, for luggage only. The Isole Ambientali inside are closed around the clock.

When it's on

Mon – Sun7:30 am – 7:30 pm

Day ticket

€10.00

Activate it before you drive in. GZ day ticket €10, or BZ €5 for 2 hours; parking in the ZTL's paid spaces included. Buy and activate it before you pass the camera: Tap&Park app, the Infomobility website, the parking meters next to the gates, Infomobility's desk (Viale Mentana 29/A) or partner tobacconists. Forgot? A €15 ROZ ticket bought by 23:59 the next day regularises the entry (parking not included). It does not open the Isole Ambientali.

Low-emission zone

Smog limits · Inside the ring roads

Mon – Fri nights (1 Oct – 7 Dec), 8:30 am – 6:30 pmNot reviewed yet
ZTL

From 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027, Monday to Friday 8:30 am–6:30 pm, the oldest cars can't drive inside the ring roads: petrol, LPG and methane up to Euro 2, diesel up to Euro 4. Electric and hybrid cars are exempt. Not on 8 and 25 December, 1 and 6 January. On emergency days it runs 8 am–7 pm and includes diesel Euro 5. Fine €168–679.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

How much is the fine for entering the Parma ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Parma ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (ZTL 1, ZTL 2, ZTL 3, Isola Ambientale, Isola Ambientale, Isola Ambientale and Isola Ambientale). Write to the hotel before you arrive.

Is there a day ticket for the Parma ZTL?

Can I drive a rental car into Smog limits?

A typical modern rental car (petrol Euro 6, hybrid or electric) can drive in. Older cars banned while it's on: petrol up to Euro 2 (since 1 October 2026); LPG up to Euro 2 (since 1 October 2026); diesel up to Euro 4 (since 1 October 2026). Electric cars: always free.
