ZTL · no entry without a permit

Città Antica · Historic Centre ZTL

Closed to unauthorised cars 24 hours a day, every day, holidays included: since 28 October 2024 there are no free-entry hours. There is no day pass for visitors. If your hotel is inside the ZTL, it must register your plate before you drive in; holiday rentals can't. Motorbikes and scooters may enter.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

It is therefore necessary, in order to enter the ZTL, to stay overnight at a hotel located inside the ZTL area; simply being a tourist is not enough.

  • Residents of Verona and its province can book up to 4 entries a month (Mon–Fri 10–18 and 20–23, weekends and holidays 10–13:30). This is not open to other visitors.
  • Hotel guests: only hotels and other classified accommodation inside the ZTL can register your plate, in advance; holiday rentals (locazioni turistiche) cannot. No deadline after entry was found.
  • Full-electric cars (not hybrids) may enter after registering online for 12 months, but the portal requires an Italian SPID or CIE digital identity.
  • Disabled badge holders: the plate must be registered; for an occasional entry, within 72 hours on accessiztl.comune.verona.it.
  • New ZTL Santo Stefano (Veronetta, across the river): fines from Monday 2 November 2026 (city). Hours every day 9–12:30, 14–17:30 and 20–7, confirmed only by local newspapers (Verona Tomorrow, L'Arena). Not on the map yet.
  • Cameras since 21/09/2026: Piazza Bra is closed to cars 24 hours a day, holidays included; the reserved lane on Ponte Aleardi is closed 7–18 every day. Not on the map yet.
  • Fine: €98.50, or €73.60 if paid within 5 days, according to an older Local Police FAQ page (archive); the current amount may be higher.
  • Not modelled: the pedestrian area of Piazza Erbe (closed even with most permits), the stadium ZTL on match days, and the winter anti-smog bans (1 Oct–30 Apr) for older vehicles across the whole city.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

How much is the fine for entering the Verona ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Verona ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Città Antica, Città Antica and Riva San Lorenzo). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
