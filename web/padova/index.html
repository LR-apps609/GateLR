ZTL · no entry without a permit

Zona B · Squares and Riverside

Every day, all day, closedNot reviewed yet
ZTL

Closed to unauthorised cars 24 hours a day, every day, holidays included (€80 fine). Motorbikes and mopeds may enter. There is no day ticket for tourists. If your hotel is inside, it can register your plate within 48 hours, for loading and unloading only. Many streets here are also pedestrian areas, closed even with a ZTL permit.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

Motorbikes and scooters: always free.

Car-free Sundays · Inside the walls

Sun (8 Nov – 8 Nov), 8:30 am – 6:30 pmNot reviewed yet
ZTL

Car-free Sundays inside the 16th-century walls: 8 November and 6 December 2026, 24 January, 28 February, 14 March and 18 April 2027, 8:30 am–6:30 pm. Only fully electric cars may drive; petrol, diesel and hybrid cars may not. Hotel guests inside may drive to and from the hotel on the day they arrive or leave, carrying a copy of the booking. €87 fine.

Low-emission zone

Padua · Winter smog limits

Mon – Fri (1 Oct – 11 Dec), 8:30 am – 7:30 pmNot reviewed yet
ZTL

From 1 October to 11 December 2026 and from 7 January to 30 April 2027, Monday to Friday 8:30 am–7:30 pm (not on public holidays), the oldest cars can't drive anywhere in Padua: diesel up to Euro 4, petrol, LPG and methane up to Euro 1. Electric and hybrid cars are exempt. The ring roads and motorways are excluded. On PM10 alert days (orange or red) the ban is stricter and also applies at weekends. €168 fine.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

How much is the fine for entering the Padua ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Padua ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Zona B and Zona A). Write to the hotel before you arrive.

Can I drive a rental car into Padua?

A typical modern rental car (petrol Euro 6, hybrid or electric) can drive in. Older cars banned while it's on: petrol up to Euro 1 (since 1 October 2026); LPG up to Euro 1 (since 1 October 2026); diesel up to Euro 4 (since 1 October 2026). Electric cars: always free.
