Old town · pedestrian area

⚠ Unofficial source: always check the sign at the gate

The old town of Polignano a Mare (through the Arco Marchesale gate, from Piazza Vittorio Emanuele to Piazza Santa Maria delle Grazie and Via Tritone up to Via Narciso) is a pedestrian area closed to cars and motorbikes 24 hours a day, every day. Camera at the Arco Marchesale. Park outside and walk in. Rules from an unofficial site.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Polignano a Mare ZTL active?

Old town: The old town of Polignano a Mare (through the Arco Marchesale gate, from Piazza Vittorio Emanuele to Piazza Santa Maria delle Grazie and Via Tritone up to Via Narciso) is a pedestrian area closed to cars and motorbikes 24 hours a day, every day. Camera at the Arco Marchesale. Park outside and walk in. Rules from an unofficial site.

How much is the fine for entering the Polignano a Mare ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Polignano a Mare ZTL. Can I drive there?

Hotels can't register your plate here: park outside the zone and walk.
