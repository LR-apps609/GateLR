ZTL · no entry without a permit

Lecce old town · ZTL inside the walls

The old town inside the walls is closed to cars without a permit on Monday–Saturday from 1 pm to 4:30 pm and from 8 pm to 10 am, and all day on Sundays and holidays; on Monday–Saturday 10 am–1 pm and 4:30–8 pm the cameras are off. Porta Napoli, via Trinchese and via Biccari stay closed around the clock. Motorbikes and mopeds may always enter. Staying inside? The hotel or B&B registers your plate (before arrival or within 48 hours) for check-in and check-out only, with 20 minutes to unload.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

Official source

City of Lecce · Official page ↗

“I varchi elettronici di controllo degli ingressi nel centro storico della città saranno spenti dalle ore 10:00 alle ore 13:00 e dalle ore 16:30 alle ore 20:00 nei giorni feriali mentre l’area rimane inaccessibile nei giorni festivi e di domenica, così come sarà vietato transitare dal varco di Porta Napoli H24 tutti i giorni della settimana .”

The electronic gates into the old town will be switched off from 10:00 to 13:00 and from 16:30 to 20:00 on working days, while the area stays closed on holidays and Sundays; driving through the Porta Napoli gate will be banned 24 hours a day, every day of the week.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Lecce ZTL active?

Lecce old town: The old town inside the walls is closed to cars without a permit on Monday–Saturday from 1 pm to 4:30 pm and from 8 pm to 10 am, and all day on Sundays and holidays; on Monday–Saturday 10 am–1 pm and 4:30–8 pm the cameras are off. Porta Napoli, via Trinchese and via Biccari stay closed around the clock. Motorbikes and mopeds may always enter. Staying inside? The hotel or B&B registers your plate (before arrival or within 48 hours) for check-in and check-out only, with 20 minutes to unload.

How much is the fine for entering the Lecce ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Lecce ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Lecce old town). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
