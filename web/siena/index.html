ZTL

Closed to unauthorised vehicles 24 hours a day, every day, holidays included. If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Siena ZTL active?

Siena: Closed to unauthorised vehicles 24 hours a day, every day, holidays included. There is no tourist day pass: short permits are only for specific needs (heavy items, passengers over 70 or under 6, pets to the vet). If your hotel is inside, it can register your plate for luggage only: max 45 minutes, twice a day.

How much is the fine for entering the Siena ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Siena ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Siena). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
