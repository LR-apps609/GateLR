ZTL · no entry without a permit

Teatro Massimo · historic-centre ZTL

The small ZTL around the Teatro Massimo Bellini (piazza Scammacca, via Pulvirenti, piazza Ogninella) is closed to cars without a permit 24 hours a day, every day; cameras at the via Sant'Agata and via Landolina entrances off via Vittorio Emanuele II. Staying inside? Your hotel or B&B can get a free 12-hour permit for your car on the AMTS website. Via Etnea and piazza Bellini are pedestrian.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Catania ZTL active?

Teatro Massimo: The small ZTL around the Teatro Massimo Bellini (piazza Scammacca, via Pulvirenti, piazza Ogninella) is closed to cars without a permit 24 hours a day, every day; cameras at the via Sant'Agata and via Landolina entrances off via Vittorio Emanuele II. Staying inside? Your hotel or B&B can get a free 12-hour permit for your car on the AMTS website. Via Etnea and piazza Bellini are pedestrian.

How much is the fine for entering the Catania ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Catania ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Teatro Massimo). Write to the hotel before you arrive.

Can I drive into the pedestrian areas of Catania?

No. Cars can't drive into pedestrian areas at any time, not even with a permit or a ticket. Park outside and walk.
