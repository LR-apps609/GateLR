Low-emission zone

Scudo Verde · Low-emission zone

Every day, all day, closedNot reviewed yet
ZTL

Most of the city, inside a ring of 77 cameras (the airport and Careggi hospital are outside). Active 24 hours a day, every day. Petrol Euro 0 and diesel Euro 0–1 cars can't drive in; from 1 December 2026 also petrol Euro 1 and diesel Euro 2. Other cars enter free, no ticket.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Older cars banned while it's on

A typical modern rental car (petrol Euro 6, hybrid or electric) can drive in.

  • petrol up to Euro 0 · since 1 July 2025
  • diesel up to Euro 1 · since 1 July 2025
  • petrol up to Euro 1 · from 1 December 2026
  • diesel up to Euro 2 · from 1 December 2026

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Florence ZTL active?

Sector A: On weekdays you can't drive in from 7:30 am to 8 pm without a permit, on Saturdays until 4 pm. In summer it's also closed on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights, 11 pm to 3 am. Motorbikes and scooters may enter. Sector B: On weekdays you can't drive in from 7:30 am to 8 pm without a permit, on Saturdays until 4 pm. In summer it's also closed on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights, 11 pm to 3 am. Motorbikes and scooters may enter. Sector O: On weekdays you can't drive in from 7:30 am to 8 pm without a permit, on Saturdays until 4 pm. In summer it's also closed on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights, 11 pm to 3 am. Motorbikes and scooters may enter. Sector F: Only on summer nights: from the first Thursday of April to the first Sunday of October, Thursday, Friday and Saturday from 11 pm to 3 am. The rest of the year it's open to all. Motorbikes and scooters may enter. Sector G: Only on summer nights: from the first Thursday of April to the first Sunday of October, Thursday, Friday and Saturday from 11 pm to 3 am. The rest of the year it's open to all. Motorbikes and scooters may enter. Scudo Verde: Most of the city, inside a ring of 77 cameras (the airport and Careggi hospital are outside). Active 24 hours a day, every day. Petrol Euro 0 and diesel Euro 0–1 cars can't drive in; from 1 December 2026 also petrol Euro 1 and diesel Euro 2. Other cars enter free, no ticket.

How much is the fine for entering the Florence ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Florence ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Sector A, Sector B and Sector O). Write to the hotel before you arrive.

Can I drive a rental car into Scudo Verde?

A typical modern rental car (petrol Euro 6, hybrid or electric) can drive in. Older cars banned while it's on: petrol up to Euro 0 (since 1 July 2025); diesel up to Euro 1 (since 1 July 2025); petrol up to Euro 1 (from 1 December 2026); diesel up to Euro 2 (from 1 December 2026).

Can I drive into the pedestrian areas of Florence?

No. Cars can't drive into pedestrian areas at any time, not even with a permit or a ticket. Park outside and walk.
