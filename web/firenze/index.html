ZTL · no entry without a permit

Sector A · Historic Centre

On weekdays you can't drive in from 7:30 am to 8 pm without a permit, on Saturdays until 4 pm. In summer it's also closed on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights, 11 pm to 3 am.

When it's on

Sat7:30 am – 4 pm

Sundayopen to all

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Florence ZTL active?

Sector A: On weekdays you can't drive in from 7:30 am to 8 pm without a permit, on Saturdays until 4 pm. In summer it's also closed on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights, 11 pm to 3 am. Sector B: On weekdays you can't drive in from 7:30 am to 8 pm without a permit, on Saturdays until 4 pm. In summer it's also closed on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights, 11 pm to 3 am. Sector O: On weekdays you can't drive in from 7:30 am to 8 pm without a permit, on Saturdays until 4 pm. In summer it's also closed on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights, 11 pm to 3 am. Sector F: Only on summer nights: from early April to the first weekend of October, Thursday, Friday and Saturday from 11 pm to 3 am. The rest of the year it's open to all. Sector G: Only on summer nights: from early April to the first weekend of October, Thursday, Friday and Saturday from 11 pm to 3 am. The rest of the year it's open to all.

How much is the fine for entering the Florence ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Florence ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Sector A, Sector B and Sector O). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
