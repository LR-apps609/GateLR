Historic centre · ZTL A

⚠ Unofficial source: always check the sign at the gate

Mantua's historic centre (Piazza delle Erbe, Sant'Andrea, Piazza Sordello and the Ducal Palace area) is ZTL A, reportedly camera-controlled every day, 24 hours a day. Without a permit stay out: park outside (e.g. Campo Canoa, Piazza Virgiliana) and walk. Staying inside? Ask your hotel to register your plate; visitors can buy an hourly permit online from Aster.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

“Le telecamere a protezione della ZTL A e B di Mantova sono attive tutti i giorni 24 su 24 ore”

The cameras protecting ZTL A and B in Mantua are active every day, 24 hours a day.

  • Aster: access to the ZTL (passes)
  • Aster: hourly ZTL permit for visitors
  • Aster: goods vehicles in ZTL A and B (6–10, 15–16:30)
  • City of Mantua: disabled badge holders from other cities

Hotel guests: the hotel records your plate on a permit for loading and unloading luggage, valid 24 hours, parking at most 60 minutes (confirmed only by accessibilitacentristorici.it, old rules); hotels and the Gazzetta di Mantova mention online registration through Aster.
  • Electric cars are not exempt: drivers from other municipalities need a transit-only pass (100 €/year, Aster). Blue Badge holders from other cities book their access with the City (form to infomulte@comune.mantova.it). Pedestrian sub-zones inside ZTL A: Via Roma, Corso Umberto I, Via Grazioli, Piazza Erbe, Piazza Broletto, Via Verdi (bollard).

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Mantua ZTL active?

Historic centre: Mantua's historic centre (Piazza delle Erbe, Sant'Andrea, Piazza Sordello and the Ducal Palace area) is ZTL A, reportedly camera-controlled every day, 24 hours a day. Without a permit stay out: park outside (e.g. Campo Canoa, Piazza Virgiliana) and walk. Staying inside? Ask your hotel to register your plate; visitors can buy an hourly permit online from Aster. Via Carducci and Via Speri: ZTL B covers the streets west of Corso della Libertà around Via Carducci, Via Tito Speri and Via Frutta, reportedly camera-controlled every day, 24 hours a day. Without a permit stay out. San Leonardo: ZTL N is the small quarter around Piazza San Leonardo and Via Cavriani, north-west of the centre, reportedly camera-controlled every day, 24 hours a day. Without a permit stay out.

How much is the fine for entering the Mantua ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Mantua ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Historic centre, Via Carducci and Via Speri and San Leonardo). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
