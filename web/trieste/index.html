  • «Type B» ZTLs are reserved 0:00–24:00 on working days, mainly for residents' parking (municipal police FAQ). GateLR treats Monday–Saturday as working days and Sundays and public holidays as free; the City does not spell out whether Saturday counts.
  • No cameras are known in Trieste: checks are made by municipal police officers. Not in GateLR yet: the small «type A» ZTLs (Via Genova, Caboro, Beccherie/Ombrelle/Ponte/Pane, reserved mainly for pedestrians, no official hours) and the many pedestrian areas (e.g. Piazza Unità d'Italia, Cavana).

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

How much is the fine for entering the Trieste ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Trieste ZTL. Can I drive there?

Hotels can't register your plate here: park outside the zone and walk.
