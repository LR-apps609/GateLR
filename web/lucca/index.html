ZTL · no entry without a permit

Sector A · Pedestrian centre ZTL

The pedestrian heart of the old town (Piazza San Michele, Piazza Napoleone, Piazza dell'Anfiteatro, via Fillungo): closed to unauthorised vehicles at all times, every day, holidays included. There is no day permit for visitors, electric cars included. If your hotel or B&B is inside sector A or B, it can register your plate for free (within 72 hours of your passing the camera): during your stay you may drive in by the shortest route and stop for check-in and luggage for up to 30 minutes with a parking disc; then park outside sectors A and B or in the hotel's own parking.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

ZTL · no entry without a permit

Sector B · Historic centre ZTL

Most of the old town around sector A: you may enter only with a permit. The rules give no time window for sector B, so it applies 24 hours a day, every day, holidays included. The blue-line car parks inside the walls (sector C) stay reachable for visitors. There is no day permit for visitors, electric cars included. If your hotel or B&B is inside sector A or B, it can register your plate for free (within 72 hours of your passing the camera): during your stay you may drive in by the shortest route and stop for check-in and luggage for up to 30 minutes with a parking disc; then park outside sectors A and B or in the hotel's own parking.

Motorbikes and scooters: always free.

Sector B · Villa Bottini block (cameras suspended)

The block between via della Quarquonia, via dei Bacchettoni, via Elisa and via Santa Chiara is sector B on the official map: entry only with a permit. Its cameras have been switched off since January 2026 because of the Villa Bottini works, and the city has not said when they come back: GateLR treats it as closed, to be safe. The streets around it lead freely to the Piazza San Ponziano and via dei Bacchettoni car parks. If your hotel or B&B is inside sector A or B, it can register your plate for free (within 72 hours of your passing the camera): during your stay you may drive in by the shortest route and stop for check-in and luggage for up to 30 minutes with a parking disc; then park outside sectors A and B or in the hotel's own parking.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

How much is the fine for entering the Lucca ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Lucca ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Sector A, Sector B and Sector B). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
