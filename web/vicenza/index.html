ZTL

The old town (Piazza dei Signori, Basilica Palladiana, Corso Palladio) is a limited traffic zone: cameras at the entry gates record every car 24 hours a day and fine those without a permit automatically. There is no permit for visitors: park outside the zone and walk in.

Official source

City of Vicenza · Official page ↗

“Le telecamere registrano tutti gli accessi dei veicoli 24 ore su 24, tranne che per il varco di corso Fogazzaro (varco CHIUSO, con rilevazione dei transiti non autorizzati, dalle 20 alle 9 nei giorni feriali e 0-24 nei giorni festivi).”

The cameras record every vehicle entering 24 hours a day, except at the Corso Fogazzaro gate (gate CLOSED, with detection of unauthorised transits, from 8 pm to 9 am on weekdays and all day on holidays).

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Vicenza ZTL active?

Centro storico: The old town (Piazza dei Signori, Basilica Palladiana, Corso Palladio) is a limited traffic zone: cameras at the entry gates record every car 24 hours a day and fine those without a permit automatically. There is no permit for visitors: park outside the zone and walk in.

How much is the fine for entering the Vicenza ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Vicenza ZTL. Can I drive there?

Hotels can't register your plate here: park outside the zone and walk.
