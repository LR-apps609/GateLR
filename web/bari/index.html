ZTL · no entry without a permit

Bari Vecchia · Old Town ZTL

Closed to cars without a permit 24 hours a day, every day, holidays included; seven cameras watch the entrances. Permits are only for old-town residents and a few trades, and electric and hybrid cars need one too. There is no tourist pass and hotels inside can't register your plate: park outside (blue bays on the seafront and corso De Tullio) and walk in. Blue Badge holders: call the Polizia Locale on 800 060 780 before entering, giving the gate, plate and badge number.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels can't register your plate for this zone.

Official source

City of Bari · Official page ↗

“Sulle “STRADE AZZURRE” il transito è consentito tutti i giorni dalle ore 0,00 alle ore 24,00 : - ai veicoli al servizio di persone con capacità di deambulazione sensibilmente ridotta dotati dell’apposito tagliando per disabili; - ai veicoli dei residenti, domiciliati o dimoranti nella Città Vecchia, dotati di apposito permesso.”

On the 'blue streets' driving is allowed every day from 0:00 to 24:00 only to vehicles serving people with seriously reduced mobility that show the disabled badge, and to vehicles of residents of the Old Town with a permit.

  • Perimeter and camera positions from OpenStreetMap, to be checked against the official map. The outline runs along the boundary roads (corso Vittorio Emanuele II, corso De Tullio, the seafront), which are outside the ZTL.
  • The 2023 ordinance still let all cars use two short routes by the Castle on working days 6 am–9 pm. The city abolished them (council decision, November 2025) and switched on the Castle camera on 1 July 2026: confirmed only by the local press quoting the city (BariToday, Telebari); the new decision itself was not found, and AMTAB's page still describes the old routes.
  • Piazzale Mincuzzi by the Castle: the city says the Santa Chiara entrance stays open to everyone to drive round the square (paid bays on its outer edge, working days 8:30 am–8:30 pm). GateLR shows the whole old town as closed. Confirmed only by Telebari (11 June 2026).
  • Temporary entry for visitors: the 2022 rules allow one-day permits only in 'particular and justifiable circumstances' (€3 a day); the city told hotels (Gazzetta del Mezzogiorno, 2 Sep 2026) that a paid temporary entry must be requested and granted before driving in. How to apply and today's price are not published: ask the Polizia Locale.
  • Motorbikes and mopeds may use the old town's 'green streets'; the ordinance doesn't say clearly whether they need a permit at the cameras. GateLR answers as for cars.
  • Fine: the ordinance applies article 7(14) of the Highway Code (nationally about €83–332 plus costs); Bari's sources don't state the amount.
  • Not shown: extra closures in the old town for the feast of St Nicholas (7–9 May and 6 December, set by yearly ordinances), and the pedestrian streets of the Murattiano (via Sparano, via Argiro), closed to cars. No ZTL was found in the Murattiano or the Madonnella.
If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Bari ZTL active?

Bari Vecchia: Closed to cars without a permit 24 hours a day, every day, holidays included; seven cameras watch the entrances. Permits are only for old-town residents and a few trades, and electric and hybrid cars need one too. There is no tourist pass and hotels inside can't register your plate: park outside (blue bays on the seafront and corso De Tullio) and walk in. Blue Badge holders: call the Polizia Locale on 800 060 780 before entering, giving the gate, plate and badge number.

How much is the fine for entering the Bari ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Bari ZTL. Can I drive there?

Hotels can't register your plate here: park outside the zone and walk.
