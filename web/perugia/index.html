ZTL · no entry without a permit

Acropolis · Historic centre

The hilltop centre (Corso Vannucci, Piazza IV Novembre) is closed to unauthorised vehicles from midnight to 1 pm Monday to Friday, and from midnight to 7 am on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays. In the afternoon and evening you may drive in. There is no tourist ticket: park below (Piazza Partigiani, escalators) or take the Minimetrò. Hotels inside register your plate.

When it's on

Mon – Fri12 am – 1 pm
Sat, Sun12 am – 7 am
Public holidaysas on Sunday

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Perugia ZTL active?

Acropolis: The hilltop centre (Corso Vannucci, Piazza IV Novembre) is closed to unauthorised vehicles from midnight to 1 pm Monday to Friday, and from midnight to 7 am on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays. In the afternoon and evening you may drive in. There is no tourist ticket: park below (Piazza Partigiani, escalators) or take the Minimetrò. Hotels inside register your plate. Corso Garibaldi: Corso Garibaldi and the streets around it are closed to unauthorised vehicles from midnight to 1 pm on weekdays and from midnight to 7 am on Sundays and holidays. In the afternoon and evening you may drive in. Via del Roscetto: Via del Roscetto is open to traffic only from 4 pm to 8 pm, every day. From 8 pm to 4 pm the next day it is closed to unauthorised vehicles. Corso Bersaglieri: Corso Bersaglieri is closed to unauthorised vehicles from 2 pm to 9 pm on weekdays and all day on Sundays and holidays. Via Bontempi: Via Bontempi and the streets off it are closed to traffic and parking 24 hours a day, every day (camera at Piazza Piccinino). Only residents with their permit, loading vehicles with authorisation (weekdays 7–10 am) and emergency or disabled-badge vehicles may enter.

How much is the fine for entering the Perugia ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Perugia ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Acropolis, Corso Garibaldi, Via del Roscetto and Corso Bersaglieri). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
