Walled town · Historic Centre ZTL

Every day, all day, closedNot reviewed yet
ZTL

The whole walled town (UNESCO site) is a ZTL with 6 camera gates. Closed to unauthorised cars all day on Sundays and holidays, and on Monday–Saturday except 5:30–9:30 am (Thursdays, market day, until 8:30). Park outside the walls (P1 Giubileo, P2 Montemaggio, P3/P4 Bagnaia) and walk. If your hotel is inside, it must register your plate on the Local Police portal beforehand: guests may then drive in from 3 to 8 pm to stop (not park) in the side streets, Via del Castello and Piazza della Cisterna; at night (8 pm–5:30 am) the hotel can register you within 24 hours. Disabled badge holders: plate to be registered within 48 hours. Campers and coaches are banned; speed limit 30 km/h.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

Official source

City of San Gimignano · Official page ↗

“La ZTL ha validità tutti i giorni ad eccezione dei giorni feriali nell’orario 05:30/09:30, salvo il giovedì con orario 05:30/08:30, nei giorni di fiere e per particolari esigenze o per particolari periodi dell’anno.”

The ZTL applies every day except on working days from 05:30 to 09:30 – on Thursdays 05:30 to 08:30 – on fair days and for special needs or particular periods of the year.

  • Hours: the rules (Disciplinare) say the ZTL applies every day except on working days (Monday–Saturday) from 5:30 to 9:30 am (Thursday, market day, and fair days only until 8:30). The city's press release of 27/04/2026 says the cameras are on "generally every day from 9:30 am to 5:30 am the next day", while the April 2026 brochure says "24 hours a day". So on Sundays and holidays the ZTL applies all day, but early in the morning there may be no camera. The brochure presents the 5:30–9:30 window mainly for residents and hotel guests: to be confirmed with the Local Police whether every visitor may drive in then.
  • Fair days (31 January, feast of St Gimignano; 12 March, St Fina; the Monday after the first Sunday of August; 29 August): the free morning ends at 8:30 and the main streets are closed to hotel guests until 8 pm. They are treated as fair days, not as public holidays (the rules list them separately from holidays); the hotel section once writes "31 MARZO" instead of 12 March, probably a typo.
  • Fine amount: not published by the city; ZTL violations fall under art. 7 of the Italian Highway Code.
  • Perimeter and gates: taken automatically from the city's official map (PDF image, 1:2000), aligned to OpenStreetMap buildings; accuracy a few metres. To the east the map's blue line leaves the frame where Via Bonda meets Via Porta Pisana: the zone is cut there at the edge of the map (a few metres). Gate directions (2 entry-only, 2 exit-only, 2 both ways) are confirmed only by CityNext (25/06/2025); they match the one-way streets in the rules.
  • Car parks outside the walls: P1 Giubileo, P2 Montemaggio, P3 and P4 Bagnaia (names from the city's press release of March 2026 and its parking page); visitor prices are not on an official page we could read. Until 22 November 2026 part of the pedestrian area in front of Porta San Giovanni is closed for restoration works (ordinance 109/2026); the ZTL rules do not change.
If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the San Gimignano ZTL active?

Walled town: The whole walled town (UNESCO site) is a ZTL with 6 camera gates. Closed to unauthorised cars all day on Sundays and holidays, and on Monday–Saturday except 5:30–9:30 am (Thursdays, market day, until 8:30). Park outside the walls (P1 Giubileo, P2 Montemaggio, P3/P4 Bagnaia) and walk. If your hotel is inside, it must register your plate on the Local Police portal beforehand: guests may then drive in from 3 to 8 pm to stop (not park) in the side streets, Via del Castello and Piazza della Cisterna; at night (8 pm–5:30 am) the hotel can register you within 24 hours. Disabled badge holders: plate to be registered within 48 hours. Campers and coaches are banned; speed limit 30 km/h.

How much is the fine for entering the San Gimignano ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the San Gimignano ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Walled town). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
