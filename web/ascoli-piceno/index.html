ZTL · no entry without a permit

Corso Mazzini east · wide-hours ZTL

Palazzo Malaspina, Corso Mazzini (east end), Via Palestro, Via dei Centini Piccolomini. Limited traffic zone with cameras: closed without a permit 9:00–12:45 and 15:30–5:00 every day; open to everyone 5:00–9:00 and 12:45–15:30. The screen at each gate shows «ZTL Attiva» (red) or «ZTL non Attiva» (green).

When it's on

Mon – Sun9 am – 12:45 pm
Mon – Sun3:30 pm – 5 am

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

Motorbikes and scooters: always free.

2 camera gates

Official source

City of Ascoli Piceno · Official page ↗

“Per le zone a traffico limitato esistenti, perimetrate con Deliberazione n. 123/2015 e successiva modifica con Deliberazione n. 37/2023 il transito veicolare è limitato ai soli autorizzati dalle ore 9:00 alle ore 12:45 e dalle ore 15:30 alle ore 05:00”

For the existing limited traffic zones, defined by resolution 123/2015 as amended by resolution 37/2023, traffic is limited to authorised vehicles only from 9:00 to 12:45 and from 15:30 to 05:00

  • Ordinance 713 of 30/10/2025: reduced-hours ZTL weekdays 8:30 pm–5 am, holidays all day (experimental, from 3/11/2025)

  • Council resolution 37/2023: ZTL and pedestrian-area rules with the street lists (PDF)

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

How much is the fine for entering the Ascoli Piceno ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Ascoli Piceno ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Corso Mazzini east, Canterine, Vidacilio, Ventidio Basso and Corso Trento e Trieste). Write to the hotel before you arrive.

Can I drive into the pedestrian areas of Ascoli Piceno?

No. Cars can't drive into pedestrian areas at any time, not even with a permit or a ticket. Park outside and walk.
