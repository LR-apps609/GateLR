ZTL · no entry without a permit

Old town · ZTL 24 hours

The old town of Bolzano (Piazza Walther, Via Portici, Piazza Erbe) is closed to cars without authorisation 24 hours a day, every day; cameras at the entrances read plates. There is no day ticket: if your hotel is inside, contact it before arriving and give your plate. Otherwise park outside (e.g. Mayr-Nusser car park, Piazza Walther underground car park).

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Bolzano ZTL active?

Old town: The old town of Bolzano (Piazza Walther, Via Portici, Piazza Erbe) is closed to cars without authorisation 24 hours a day, every day; cameras at the entrances read plates. There is no day ticket: if your hotel is inside, contact it before arriving and give your plate. Otherwise park outside (e.g. Mayr-Nusser car park, Piazza Walther underground car park).

How much is the fine for entering the Bolzano ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Bolzano ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Old town). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
