ZTL · no entry without a permit

Old town · ZTL inside the walls

Volterra's old town inside the walls (Piazza dei Priori, the Duomo) is closed to cars without a permit every day, holidays included: 10 am–8 pm from 21 March to 1 November, 10 am–5:30 pm from 2 November to 20 March. Cameras at Porta San Francesco and Porta a Selci. Outside those hours you can drive in. Park outside the walls (e.g. La Dogana under Piazza Martiri, Docciola, La Stazione) and walk.

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Volterra ZTL active?

Old town: Volterra's old town inside the walls (Piazza dei Priori, the Duomo) is closed to cars without a permit every day, holidays included: 10 am–8 pm from 21 March to 1 November, 10 am–5:30 pm from 2 November to 20 March. Cameras at Porta San Francesco and Porta a Selci. Outside those hours you can drive in. Park outside the walls (e.g. La Dogana under Piazza Martiri, Docciola, La Stazione) and walk.

How much is the fine for entering the Volterra ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Volterra ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Old town). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
