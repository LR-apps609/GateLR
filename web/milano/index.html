ZTL · entry with a day ticket

Area C · City Centre

A paid zone: from Monday to Friday, 7:30 am to 7:30 pm, you can drive in with a €7.50 ticket, activated by midnight of the next day. Public holidays are free.

When it's on

Mon – Fri7:30 am – 7:30 pm
Saturday, Sundayopen to all
Public holidaysopen to all

Day ticket

€7.50

Pay by midnight of the next day. Pay and activate it by midnight of the next day (online, at newsagents or by phone). Later, up to the 7th day, it costs €22.50. Parked in a partner garage: €4.50.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Milan ZTL active?

Area C: A paid zone: from Monday to Friday, 7:30 am to 7:30 pm, you can drive in with a €7.50 ticket, activated by midnight of the next day. Public holidays are free. Area B: Almost the whole city. From Monday to Friday, 7:30 am to 7:30 pm (holidays excluded), the most polluting vehicles can't drive in. Cleaner cars enter free, no ticket.

How much is the fine for entering the Milan ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Milan ZTL. Can I drive there?

Hotels can't register your plate here, but you can enter with the day ticket.

Is there a day ticket for the Milan ZTL?

Area C: €7.50, pay by midnight of the next day. Pay and activate it by midnight of the next day (online, at newsagents or by phone). Later, up to the 7th day, it costs €22.50. Parked in a partner garage: €4.50.

Can I drive a rental car into Area B?

A typical modern rental car (petrol Euro 6, hybrid or electric) can drive in. Older cars banned while it's on: diesel up to Euro 5 (since 25 February 2019); petrol up to Euro 3 (since 1 October 2025); petrol up to Euro 4 (from 1 October 2028); diesel up to Euro 6 (from 1 October 2028). Electric cars: always free.

Can I drive into the pedestrian areas of Milan?

No. Cars can't drive into pedestrian areas at any time, not even with a permit or a ticket. Park outside and walk.
