ZTL · no entry without a permit

Ortigia · island ZTL

Ortigia (south of piazza Pancali and the Temple of Apollo) is closed to cars without a permit Monday–Saturday 11 am–3:30 pm and 5 pm–2 am, and on Sundays and holidays from 10 am to 2 am. Mornings until 11 and 3:30–5 pm on weekdays you may drive in. Staying in Ortigia? Your hotel or B&B registers your plate within 48 hours, only to unload. Park at Talete (north tip) or Molo Sant'Antonio and walk.

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Syracuse ZTL active?

Ortigia: Ortigia (south of piazza Pancali and the Temple of Apollo) is closed to cars without a permit Monday–Saturday 11 am–3:30 pm and 5 pm–2 am, and on Sundays and holidays from 10 am to 2 am. Mornings until 11 and 3:30–5 pm on weekdays you may drive in. Staying in Ortigia? Your hotel or B&B registers your plate within 48 hours, only to unload. Park at Talete (north tip) or Molo Sant'Antonio and walk.

How much is the fine for entering the Syracuse ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Syracuse ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Ortigia). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
