ZTL · no entry without a permit

Castello · hilltop quarter

Mon – Sun, 12 am – 9 am
ZTL

⚠ Unofficial source: always check the sign at the gate

Castello (the walled hilltop quarter with the Cathedral and Bastione di Saint Remy) is closed to cars and motorbikes without a pass every night from midnight to 9 am. During the day you may drive in. Hours from an unofficial site.

When it's on

Mon – Sun12 am – 9 am

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Cagliari ZTL active?

Castello: Castello (the walled hilltop quarter with the Cathedral and Bastione di Saint Remy) is closed to cars and motorbikes without a pass every night from midnight to 9 am. During the day you may drive in. Hours from an unofficial site. Marina: Marina (the quarter between Via Roma and Castello) is closed to cars and motorbikes without a pass every day 3:30–5 pm and 9 pm–7 am. Hours from an unofficial site. Villanova: Villanova (between Viale Regina Elena, Via Garibaldi and Via Bacaredda) is closed to cars and motorbikes without a pass every day 3:30–5 pm and 9 pm–7 am. Hours from an unofficial site. Stampace Alto: Upper Stampace (around Sant'Efisio and Via Ospedale) is closed to cars and motorbikes without a pass every day 3:30–5 pm and 9 pm–9 am. Hours from an unofficial site. Stampace Basso: Lower Stampace (between Via Roma, Viale Trieste and Corso Vittorio Emanuele) is closed to cars and motorbikes without a pass every day, holidays included, 7:30–10:30 am and 3:30–5 pm. Hours from an unofficial site.

How much is the fine for entering the Cagliari ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Cagliari ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Castello, Marina, Villanova, Stampace Alto and Stampace Basso). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
