The app said I could enter, but I got a fine

Road signs and the panel at each gate always win over the app. Each answer links to the city's official source and shows the date we read it; cities marked Draft are not yet reviewed by a person. Please tell us what happened so we can check the rule.

A rule or a gate is wrong

Write to us with the city, the street or gate name, and a link or photo of the sign if you have one. We re-check the official source and update the city.

Alerts don't arrive

  • Settings → GateLR → Location: choose Always.
  • Settings → GateLR → Notifications: turn them on.
  • In the app, Garage → Alerts: check that Gate alerts is on.
