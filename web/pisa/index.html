ZTL · no entry without a permit

ZTL Nord · Historic Centre north of the Arno

The north sector of Pisa's ZTL: Piazza dei Miracoli, Piazza dei Cavalieri and the streets around them. Closed to unauthorised cars 24 hours a day, every day, holidays included. There is no day ticket for tourists. Mopeds and motorcycles may enter. If you sleep in a hotel or B&B inside the ZTL, it can register your plate so you can drive there.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

“Nelle zone come definite nella prima parte è istituito il divieto di accesso, transito e sosta di tutti i veicoli, nell’orario 0-24 di tutti i giorni, fatte salve le deroghe espresse ai punti successivi.”

In the zones defined in part one, access, transit and parking are forbidden to all vehicles, 0–24 every day, except for the exemptions in the following points.

  • Hotel guests: if the hotel is inside the ZTL, it enters your plate and arrival time on the portal within 10 hours of your entry (ordinance 2386/2024, 5.1), for up to 3 days at a time, renewable; you may stop up to 30 minutes in the hotel's luggage bays. The city's 2023 notice says access to reach the hotel is free. To also park in the ZTL (not in residents' bays) and on blue lines, the hotel can sell a €12, 24-hour permit (2025 fees, one per room).

  • No day ticket for tourists: the temporary permits of ordinance 2386/2024 are only for specific cases (weddings, removals, works, taxis…). Mopeds, light quadricycles and motorcycles may enter freely. Disabled badge holders registered on the national CUDE platform are added to the white list automatically.
  • Electric cars: no exemption in the rules of ordinance 2386/2024 (its preamble only cites a 2013 council resolution on electric vehicles in the ZTL, which we have not read).

Lungarni · Weekend night ZTL

The riverside Lungarni (Pacinotti, Mediceo, Galilei, Gambacorti), Ponte di Mezzo, piazza Mazzini, piazza Carrara and via San Nicola: closed to unauthorised cars on Friday, Saturday and Sunday nights from 10 pm to 5 am. ZTL Nord and Sud permits are valid here.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Pisa ZTL active?

ZTL Nord: The north sector of Pisa's ZTL: Piazza dei Miracoli, Piazza dei Cavalieri and the streets around them. Closed to unauthorised cars 24 hours a day, every day, holidays included. There is no day ticket for tourists. Mopeds and motorcycles may enter. If you sleep in a hotel or B&B inside the ZTL, it can register your plate so you can drive there. ZTL Sud: The south sector of Pisa's ZTL: the San Martino and Sant'Antonio quarters (the riverside Lungarni are outside). Closed to unauthorised cars 24 hours a day, every day, holidays included. There is no day ticket for tourists. Mopeds and motorcycles may enter. If you sleep in a hotel or B&B inside the ZTL, it can register your plate so you can drive there. Lungarni: The riverside Lungarni (Pacinotti, Mediceo, Galilei, Gambacorti), Ponte di Mezzo, piazza Mazzini, piazza Carrara and via San Nicola: closed to unauthorised cars on Friday, Saturday and Sunday nights from 10 pm to 5 am. ZTL Nord and Sud permits are valid here.

How much is the fine for entering the Pisa ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Pisa ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (ZTL Nord and ZTL Sud). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
