ZTL · no entry without a permit

Modena · Historic centre ZTL

The historic centre inside the ring boulevards (Duomo, Piazza Grande) is closed to unauthorised cars 24 hours a day, every day; cameras check every entry. There is no tourist ticket. If your hotel is inside, it gives you a windscreen badge and registers your plate within 48 hours. Motorcycles and mopeds may enter.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

  • Hotels inside the ZTL: you may drive in and park nearby with the hotel's badge on the windscreen; the hotel sends your plate to the municipal police within 48 hours of your entry. Rental cars have no permit of their own.
  • Motorcycles and mopeds may enter without a permit (order, point B2). Fully electric cars need an authorisation first: they are not exempt automatically.
  • No ticket for tourists: daily passes are issued only on the same conditions as permanent permits. Some streets inside (Via Emilia Centro between Vicolo Squallore and Via Torre, Corso Duomo, north side of Piazza Roma) are reserved lanes closed even with a ZTL permit, and the pedestrian areas (Piazza Grande …) are not drawn separately.
  • Gate positions are not on the map yet (no open data with coordinates): every entry and exit of the perimeter is camera-controlled.
If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Modena ZTL active?

Modena: The historic centre inside the ring boulevards (Duomo, Piazza Grande) is closed to unauthorised cars 24 hours a day, every day; cameras check every entry. There is no tourist ticket. If your hotel is inside, it gives you a windscreen badge and registers your plate within 48 hours. Motorcycles and mopeds may enter.

How much is the fine for entering the Modena ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Modena ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Modena). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
