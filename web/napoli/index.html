ZTL · no entry without a permit

Centro Antico · Historic Centre ZTL

Closed without a permit every day from 9 am, public holidays included. Three gates switch off at 5 pm; the via del Sole gate and the Mezzocannone gate stay on until 10 pm Monday to Thursday and until 2 am Friday to Sunday. Motorbikes and scooters may enter (not in via Mezzocannone). Piazza del Gesù inside is a pedestrian area, closed day and night. There is no day ticket for visitors: temporary permits are only for documented needs (moving house, works, medical visits for people with walking problems), requested by email. If your hotel is inside, it can apply for a permit for its guests' cars: ask it before arriving. Disabled badge holders must have the plate registered first (or report the transit within 48 hours).

  • Weekday public holidays, Easter Monday included, follow the Sunday hours. The eves of holidays are counted only for holidays on a fixed date (Easter Monday's eve is a Sunday anyway). The city's acts don't define «festivi e prefestivi».
  • 19 September (San Gennaro, patron saint of Naples): GateLR counts it as a holiday (Sunday hours, 18 September as its eve); the city's pages and ordinances don't say so explicitly.

Tarsia · Pignasecca–Dante ZTL

Closed without a permit every day from 9 am to 6 pm, public holidays included. Via Domenico Soriano and via Brombeis inside are pedestrian areas, closed all day. Motorbikes and scooters may enter (not via Brombeis). There is no day ticket for visitors: temporary permits are only for documented needs (moving house, works, medical visits for people with walking problems), requested by email. If your hotel is inside and has parking spaces, it can register your plate before you arrive. Disabled badge holders must have the plate registered first (or report the transit within 48 hours).

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

How much is the fine for entering the Naples ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Naples ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Centro Antico, Tarsia, Belledonne and Morelli). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
