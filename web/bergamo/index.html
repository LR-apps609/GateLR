Città Alta · Historic centre

Every day, all day, closedNot reviewed yet
ZTL

The walled upper town is closed to unauthorised vehicles 24 hours a day, every day, holidays included. There is no ticket for visitors: park outside (e.g. at the walls) or take the funicular. If your hotel is inside, it can register your plate within 72 hours.

When it's on

Every day, all dayclosed

Staying at a hotel inside

Hotels inside this zone can register your plate with the city, so you can drive to the hotel. Ask them before you arrive.

Città Alta & Colli · Sundays, holidays, summer nights

Sun, 10 am – 12 amNot reviewed yet
ZTL

The roads up to Città Alta and the hills (Colli) are closed on Sundays and public holidays from 10 am to midnight, and during summer time on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights from 9 pm to 1 am. Otherwise you can drive up freely (the walled centre stays closed). While it is closed you may still drive to the Fara, Tre Armi and Porta Sant'Alessandro car parks, but only through the gate on Viale Vittorio Emanuele (Conca d'Oro roundabout). Motorbikes may enter.

When it's on

Sun10 am – 12 am
Thu – Sat nights (1 Apr – 30 Oct)9 pm – 1 am
Monday, Tuesday, Wednesdayopen to all
Public holidaysas on Sunday

Motorbikes and scooters: always free.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Bergamo ZTL active?

Città Alta: The walled upper town is closed to unauthorised vehicles 24 hours a day, every day, holidays included. There is no ticket for visitors: park outside (e.g. at the walls) or take the funicular. If your hotel is inside, it can register your plate within 72 hours. Città Alta & Colli: The roads up to Città Alta and the hills (Colli) are closed on Sundays and public holidays from 10 am to midnight, and during summer time on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights from 9 pm to 1 am. Otherwise you can drive up freely (the walled centre stays closed). While it is closed you may still drive to the Fara, Tre Armi and Porta Sant'Alessandro car parks, but only through the gate on Viale Vittorio Emanuele (Conca d'Oro roundabout). Motorbikes may enter. Città Alta & Colli: The roads up to Città Alta and the hills (Colli) are closed on Sundays and public holidays from 10 am to midnight, and during summer time on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights from 9 pm to 1 am. Otherwise you can drive up freely (the walled centre stays closed). While it is closed you may still drive to the Fara, Tre Armi and Porta Sant'Alessandro car parks, but only through the gate on Viale Vittorio Emanuele (Conca d'Oro roundabout). Motorbikes may enter. Valverde: The road from Valverde up to Porta San Lorenzo is open to everyone only Monday to Friday 7–9:30 am and 3:45–7:30 pm; the rest of the time, and all day on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays, it is closed to unauthorised vehicles. Via XX Settembre: The shopping streets of the lower town (Via XX Settembre, Via Sant'Alessandro, Piazza Pontida and nearby) are closed to unauthorised vehicles 24 hours a day. Via Tasso: Via Tasso, Piazzetta Santo Spirito and Via Pignolo between Via Verdi and Via Camozzi are closed to unauthorised vehicles 24 hours a day. Teatro Donizetti: The square in front of the Donizetti theatre (Largo Gavazzeni, Largo Belotti, Piazza Cavour and Piazza Matteotti between Via Roma and Via Tasso) is closed to unauthorised vehicles 24 hours a day. Piazza Matteotti: Piazza Matteotti (City Hall), Via Borfuro, Via Crispi and Passaggio Zeduri are closed to unauthorised vehicles 24 hours a day. Via Pelabrocco: Via Pelabrocco is closed to unauthorised vehicles 24 hours a day. Piazza Dante: Piazza Dante is a pedestrian area, closed to vehicles 24 hours a day. Piazzale Marconi: The taxi and bus lanes in front of the railway station are camera-controlled 24 hours a day: don't drive into them to drop someone off. Via Pignolo Alta: Upper Via Pignolo, Via San Tomaso and nearby streets are closed to unauthorised vehicles every night from 11 pm to 7 am. Borgo San Leonardo: Largo Cinque Vie, Via San Bernardino, Via Quarenghi and nearby streets of Borgo San Leonardo are closed to unauthorised vehicles every night from 11 pm to 7 am. Via Borgo Palazzo: Via Borgo Palazzo between Via Madonna della Neve and Via Frizzoni is closed to unauthorised vehicles every night from 11 pm to 7 am (no cameras). Via San Francesco d'Assisi: Via San Francesco d'Assisi is closed to unauthorised vehicles every night from 10 pm to 7 am (no cameras). Via Raboni: Via Raboni between Via Bassani and Via Boves is closed to unauthorised vehicles every day from 5:30 to 7:30 pm (no cameras). Via Orio: A stretch of Via Orio is open to traffic only Monday to Friday 7–9:30 am; the rest of the time it is closed to unauthorised vehicles (no cameras).

How much is the fine for entering the Bergamo ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Bergamo ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Città Alta, Città Alta & Colli, Città Alta & Colli, Valverde, Via XX Settembre, Via Tasso, Teatro Donizetti, Piazza Matteotti, Via Pelabrocco, Piazza Dante, Piazzale Marconi, Via Pignolo Alta, Borgo San Leonardo, Via Borgo Palazzo, Via San Francesco d'Assisi, Via Raboni and Via Orio). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
