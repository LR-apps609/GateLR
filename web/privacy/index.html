Privacy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

The short version

GateLR does not collect your personal data. There is no account, no tracking and no advertising.

Your location

The app uses your location only on your iPhone, to tell you which gates are near and to warn you before a gate that's on. Your location never leaves the device and we never see it.

Your cars and permits

The vehicles, plates and permits you add in Garage are saved only on your iPhone. When you send a message to your hotel, you choose the app and the recipient; GateLR does not send anything by itself.

The website

When you search a place on this website, the text you type is sent to a map search service to find the address (Photon by komoot, or Apple Maps). The map is drawn with OpenFreeMap tiles. These services may see your IP address, as with any web request. We don't store your searches.

Questions

See the support page.

Italiano: GateLR non raccoglie dati personali. Posizione, auto, targhe e permessi restano sul tuo iPhone. Nessun account, nessun tracciamento, nessuna pubblicità.
