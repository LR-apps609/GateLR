ZTL · no entry without a permit

Piazza Tasso · ZTL Centro

Mon – Sun nights (1 Apr – 25 Oct), 7:30 pm – 4 amNot reviewed yet
ZTL

From 1 April to 25 October the streets around piazza Tasso (viale Caruso, Corso Italia to via Marziale, via Correale from the Lauro car park, via De Maio, piazza Sant'Antonino, via San Francesco down to piazza della Vittoria) are closed to cars without a permit every evening 7:30 pm–4 am, and on holidays also 10 am–1 pm. From 7 December to 6 January only on holidays and the evenings before them. Staying in town? Your hotel asks the City for your permit. Park at the Lauro or Fuorimura car parks.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the Sorrento ZTL active?

Piazza Tasso: From 1 April to 25 October the streets around piazza Tasso (viale Caruso, Corso Italia to via Marziale, via Correale from the Lauro car park, via De Maio, piazza Sant'Antonino, via San Francesco down to piazza della Vittoria) are closed to cars without a permit every evening 7:30 pm–4 am, and on holidays also 10 am–1 pm. From 7 December to 6 January only on holidays and the evenings before them. Staying in town? Your hotel asks the City for your permit. Park at the Lauro or Fuorimura car parks.

How much is the fine for entering the Sorrento ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the Sorrento ZTL. Can I drive there?

Yes, if the hotel registers your plate with the city (Piazza Tasso). Write to the hotel before you arrive.
