ZTL · no entry without a permit

Centro · Historic Centre ZTL

Open to everyone only Monday to Friday from 6 to 9 am (not on public holidays). At all other times, Saturdays, Sundays and holidays included, only vehicles with a permit may enter; electric cars too. Speed limit 30 km/h. There is no ticket for tourists, and the city mentions no hotel registration. If you had to enter for a proven emergency, you can justify it with the city's form within 48 hours. Disabled badge holders may enter at any time but must first send plate and badge number by WhatsApp to the Punto Amico (+39 347 2588693).

Centro · Corso Italia

The main street (area B on the city's map), stricter than the rest of the ZTL: even permit holders may drive here only 9–11 am. For you the rule is the same: open only Monday to Friday 6–9 am (not on holidays); closed at all other times. The squares beside it (Piazza Cavour, Piazza Masaccio) are pedestrian.

If you drive in by mistake

The fine

€83–332 per day

  • €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.
  • Since December 2024 a camera-detected entry into the same ZTL is fined once per day (art. 198, para. 2-bis, Italian Highway Code).
  • Living abroad? The fine can arrive up to 360 days later. Rental companies usually add their own fee, often €25–60.
  • No penalty points on your licence.

Questions

When is the San Giovanni Valdarno ZTL active?

Centro: Open to everyone only Monday to Friday from 6 to 9 am (not on public holidays). At all other times, Saturdays, Sundays and holidays included, only vehicles with a permit may enter; electric cars too. Speed limit 30 km/h. There is no ticket for tourists, and the city mentions no hotel registration. If you had to enter for a proven emergency, you can justify it with the city's form within 48 hours. Disabled badge holders may enter at any time but must first send plate and badge number by WhatsApp to the Punto Amico (+39 347 2588693). Centro: The main street (area B on the city's map), stricter than the rest of the ZTL: even permit holders may drive here only 9–11 am. For you the rule is the same: open only Monday to Friday 6–9 am (not on holidays); closed at all other times. The squares beside it (Piazza Cavour, Piazza Masaccio) are pedestrian.

How much is the fine for entering the San Giovanni Valdarno ZTL?

€83–332 per entry and day, sent by post to the car's owner: for a rental car, that's you, plus the rental company's fee. €58.10 if you pay within 5 days (30% off the minimum), plus notification costs.

My hotel is inside the San Giovanni Valdarno ZTL. Can I drive there?

Hotels can't register your plate here: park outside the zone and walk.

Can I drive into the pedestrian areas of San Giovanni Valdarno?

No. Cars can't drive into pedestrian areas at any time, not even with a permit or a ticket. Park outside and walk.
